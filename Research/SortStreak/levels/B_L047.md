- Serve order (shelf): pink, red, green, yellow ("Great!"), blue, light blue, purple, yellow, blue/red, orange, red, ... 12 total. Light blue, purple and orange arrive with the queue.
- New ropes arrive full (5) from the right. Queue end: served slots become white pots; 2 white ropes at 12/12.
- Long middle stretch (sheet 02 frames 4-20) with many small moves among light blue / purple / blue: the hardest part.
- Praise: "Nice!", "Great!", "Unstoppable!" (partly visible), "Legendary!", "Legendary x2", "Legendary x3".
- No rejection message seen. Boosters: not visible.
- 65 steps, time 02:16 (win screen).

## 4. End of level
- Coins fly to the counter: 683 -> 695.
- "Leaderboard": own score 323 -> 335, tag "On Fire!", a banner at the top (text cut off, ends with "challenge!").
- "Superb!" flower cart, stats "Time 02:16", "Steps 65", "Win Streak 3".

## 5. Design notes
- Calm level after the L45 Hard / L46 double-vine: no mystery, no blockers, just 5 colours + 3 from the queue. Difficulty comes from look-alike blues (blue / light blue / purple) in the middle phase.
