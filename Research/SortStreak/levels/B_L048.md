# B_L048

Source: B_L048 (B playlist). 5 contact sheets (20 + 20 + 20 + 20 + 10 frames, ~225 s). Looked at: start image + sheets 01-05.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 48", no difficulty label. Coins 695 at start, 706 after the win (+11).
- IQ bar 40 / 90 / 180; badge red at the end, no "IQ GENIUS!" screen.
- Orders "0/11".
- No streak banner at the start this time (not visible).
- Booster bar: not visible (cropped), but the game text refers to boosters (see 3).

## 2. Starting board
- 7 ropes, pots: white (helper), pink, purple, lilac, red, light blue, blue.
- Rope length: 5 slots.
- Flowers top to bottom:
  - white: purple, lilac, blue, ?, lilac
  - pink: pink, ?, ?, pink
  - purple: empty
  - lilac: covered by a vine with a number badge "2"
  - red: blue, ?, ?, pink
  - light blue: lilac, purple, ?, blue, pink
  - blue: red, pink
- Colours: purple, lilac, blue, light blue, pink, red (several look-alikes: purple/lilac, blue/light blue, pink/red).
- Empty slots visible: 10.
- Specials: 6 "?" visible; 1 number vine ("2").

## 3. How it was solved
- **Attempt 1** (sheet 01): served pink (1/11), vine counted to "1", then the bottom toast "No Available Moves" / "Use a booster to continue or reset". Board dims and resets to 0/11 with the vine at "2".
- **Attempt 2** (sheets 01-02): served pink again (1/11), vine "1"; ended again with "No Available Moves" / "Use a booster to continue or reset"; reset to 0/11 (flowers fly back onto ropes).
- **Attempt 3** (sheets 02-04, won): pink, red (vine opened at 2 serves), purple, blue, lilac, ..., 11 total. Later colours from the queue: yellow, orange, green.
- Rejection toasts seen (exact): "This rope can't hold that many flowers.", "Locked flowers can't move yet. Complete the sign's goal to unlock them.", "Match the bottom flower to drop here!"
- New ropes arrive full, many with 1-3 "?". Queue end: white pots, 2 white ropes at 11/11.
- Praise: "Great!", "Excellent!", "Amazing!", "Incredible!", "Legendary x2".
- Boosters: none seen used; the player chose reset.
- Winning run: 57 steps, 01:48.

## 4. End of level
- Coins 695 -> 706.
- "Leaderboard": 340 -> 351, "On Fire!", banner "Amazing! You're at the top of the leaderboard!"
- "Unstoppable!" flower cart, stats "Time 01:48" and "Steps 57" only: **no Win Streak box** (the streak seems lost after the resets; not confirmed on screen).

## 5. Design notes
- Hardest unlabelled level in the batch: look-alike pairs + 6 "?" + a number vine -> two dead ends before the win.
- Dead end UX: a non-blocking bottom toast "No Available Moves / Use a booster to continue or reset" instead of a popup; reset is cheap.
- The vine's rejection text uses the word "sign": the vine badge is called a "sign" with a "goal".
- Win Streak box disappears from the win stats when the streak is broken (inferred).
