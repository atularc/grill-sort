# B_L034 (Level 34)

Source: B_L034 (B = Sort Streak Walkthrough playlist). 3 sheets, about 48 frames x 2.5 s = about 2 min. Frames looked at: start image, sheet01-03 (all).

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 34", no difficulty label. Coins 470 at start, 482 at the end.
- **IQ bar** above the order shelf: milestones "40", "90", an "IQ" badge, "180". It fills slowly: just started at 0/12, about between 40 and 90 at 6/12, about at/just past 90 at 12/12. The IQ badge turns red when the level is done.
- Orders counter "0/12" (now shown under the shelf).
- Banner at start: a gold trophy with flowers, "2 Streak!" / "Keep blooming!" (streak from winning L33).

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes. Pots: white (helper), light blue, red, blue, orange, lilac.
- Rope length: 5 slots.
- Flowers top to bottom:
  1. white: lilac, ?, lilac, light blue
  2. light blue: light blue, blue, light blue
  3. red: red, light blue
  4. blue: red, orange, ?, orange, blue
  5. orange: lilac, ?, orange, red, orange
  6. lilac: ?, blue
- 21 pieces in 30 slots = 9 empty slots. 4 "?" at the start. 6 colours on the start board; later: yellow, purple, pink, green also appear (about 10 colours in the level).
- No vines, locks or coin pieces in this level.

## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order (top shelf): light blue, orange, red, lilac, blue, yellow (6/12), then purple, green, pink, blue, red..., final shelf: red, lilac, blue, yellow, green, purple, blue, red, yellow, pink (12 pots; red, blue and yellow came twice).
- New ropes arrive full (5 pieces), often with 1-3 "?" pieces. When the queue ran out the ropes left one by one; at the end only the 2 white helper ropes were left (a second white helper appeared at the right near 9/12).
- Boosters: no booster bar visible.
- Messages: "This rope can't hold that many flowers.", "Match the bottom flower to drop here!". Praise: "Excellent!", "Incredible!", "Unstoppable!", "Legendary x2", "Legendary x3", "Legendary" (praise words grow with consecutive serves, with a xN combo count).
- Hesitation: sheet02 frames 2-8 show little change (player thinking around 6/12).
- Moves: about 35-40 board changes; solve time about 95 s ("Time 01:31").

## 4. End of level
- "IQ GENIUS!" popup: smiling sunflower with glasses and graduation cap in a pot on a garden patch, light bulb.
- Coins 470 -> 482 (+12) with a small pink flower icon under the coin pill.
- "Leaderboard" (timer about 36:33, podium 140 / 172 / 121; list rows "VozDoMar_01 118", "HeartSeeker 87", "VagueMediterra... 77", "ZlotyWilk 67"), then "Brilliant!" with the flower cart and "Time 01:31".

## 5. Design notes
- Easy/medium: 9 free slots, 4 "?" at the start, no blockers, 12 orders, many look-alike blues (blue / light blue) and red/pink/orange.
- New here: the IQ bar with 40/90/180 milestones and the "IQ GENIUS!" reward popup; "2 Streak! Keep blooming!" banner; combo praise "Legendary xN".
- Praise escalation (Excellent -> Incredible -> Unstoppable -> Legendary xN) rewards fast consecutive serves; worth copying.
