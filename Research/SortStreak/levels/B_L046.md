## 3. How it was solved
- First serve: green (1/14). At that moment the green-badge vine on the red rope opened, and the "2" vine changed to "1". Second serve: orange ("Nice!", 2/14) and the number vine opened. So the **number vine counts down one per served pot**.
- Later serves (shelf): green, orange, blue, light blue/purple, red, purple, pink, blue, yellow, pink, red, purple ... 14 total. Pink and yellow come from the queue.
- New ropes arrive full from the right, some with "?". Queue end: white pots replace the served slots; 2 white ropes remain at 14/14.
- Rejection toast seen once: "Match the bottom flower to drop here!"
- Praise: "Nice!", "Excellent!", "Incredible!", "Unstoppable!", "Legendary x4", "Legendary x5", "Legendary x7".
- Boosters: not visible.
- 52 steps, time 01:56 (from win screen).

## 4. End of level
- "IQ GENIUS!" sunflower screen.
- "Leaderboard": score 309 -> 323, banner "Congratulations! You're now number one!" and "Moving Up!".
- "Flawless!" flower cart (cart gets new potted flowers), stats "Time 01:56", "Steps 52", "Win Streak 3", a small line below (not readable).
- Then the cart fades to an empty green patch (transition).
- Coins 669 -> 683.

## 5. Design notes
- Two vine types in one level: colour-badge vine (opens when that colour is served) and number-badge vine (opens after N serves). First number vine in my batch.
- Win streak counter is shown at level start ("2 Streak!") and on the win screen ("Win Streak 3").
- Combo praise can go to "Legendary x7" when many pots are served back-to-back at the end.
