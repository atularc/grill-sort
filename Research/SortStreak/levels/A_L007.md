# A_L007: Level 7

Source: A_L007 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist). About 2 min 50 s (sheet01 20, sheet02 20, sheet03 20, sheet04 9 frames). Frames looked at: start + all 69 sheet frames.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 7". No difficulty label. Coins 690 during play, 800 on the win screen.
- IQ bar: none visible.
- Orders: "0/11".
- No start banner or tutorial text visible (no streak sign this time).

## 2. Starting board
- 7 ropes. Pots: white (helper), green, light blue, blue, purple, red, pink.
- Rope length 5.
- Flowers top to bottom:
  - White: ?, blue, ?, purple, pink
  - Green: green, ?, light blue, green
  - Light blue: ?, purple, red, light blue, green
  - Blue: purple
  - Purple: purple, ?, pink, ?, green
  - Red: empty
  - Pink: ?, blue, ?, light blue, green

- Empty slots: 0 + 1 + 0 + 4 + 0 + 5 + 0 = 10.

## 3. How it was solved
- Long opening: "0/11" for about 31 frames (~75 s) while "?" flowers were uncovered and stacks rearranged.
- Served order: green (1/11), red (2/11, "Nice!"), purple (3/11), pink ("Great!", 4/11), blue (5/11), light blue (6/11), red again ("Incredible!", 7/11), blue again (8/11, "Legendary!"), lavender (9/11, "Legendary!"), orange and yellow (11/11).
- Repeats: red and blue twice.
- Arrivals: full 5-flower ropes, several with "?" (orange, yellow, lavender ropes carried "?"s, e.g. 3 "?" on the board around 6/11).
- End: 2 white helper ropes left.
- Boosters: Undo ("FREE"), Hint (3), Rope (1). The Hint tile is highlighted (yellow outline) once around 0/11 but its count stays 3; no booster clearly used.
- Messages quoted: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (once, early).
- About 60 to 70 moves, ~2 min 40 s.

## 4. End of level
- "Flawless!", bouquet, fireworks/confetti, blue button "Level 8". Coins 800 (+110).
- No popup.

## 5. Design notes
- Harder than L6 without a label: more "?" (8), 7 ropes, 6 colours at start, two helper-ish empties only (blue rope 4 free, red rope empty).
- Mystery flowers create the long planning phase at the start: the player must dig to learn colours.
- Nothing new in UI or mechanics vs L6.
