# A_L027

Source: A_L027 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist). 49 sheet frames (sheet01 20, sheet02 20, sheet03 9) at 2.5 s = about 2 min of video. Frames looked at: `A_L027_start.png`, all 3 sheets.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 27", no difficulty label. Coins 3380 at start, 3489 then 3490 at the end (+110).
- This player's build looks different from B: light-blue background, Back + Restart (circular arrow) buttons top left, **no IQ bar**.
- Orders counter "0/11" above a 10-slot pot shelf (11 orders).
- Start banner (wooden sign with a "3" flower): "3 Streak! You're on a roll!"
- A small red circle "00:02" at the right edge in the first frames (looks like a timer; not explained on screen).

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes; pots: white (helper), light blue, red, green, blue, pink.
- Rope length: 5 slots.
- Flowers (top to bottom):
  - white: blue, green, blue, pink, light blue (full)
  - light blue: light blue
  - red: green, blue, red, light blue
  - green: green, red, blue, pink, red (full)
  - blue: green, red, pink, light blue
  - pink: pink, light blue, pink
- 6 colours at start (blue, light blue, green, red, pink + later yellow, orange, purple, lilac arrive). 22 flowers in 30 slots = 8 empty slots.
- No mystery "?" flowers, no blockers.

## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order on the shelf: light blue, pink, blue, red, green, yellow, lilac, purple, red, (then the last three incl. blue). Red came twice, blue twice.
- New ropes arrive from the right with their pot and a full or near-full rope (e.g. yellow, purple, orange, lilac ropes). One lilac pot was seen sliding in. When the queue emptied a second white helper pot appeared at the right end; the level ended with 2 white helper ropes.
- Boosters (bottom bar): Undo "FREE" (greyed at the very start, active after the first move), Hint (bulb) 3, Add Rope (rope coil) 1. None used (counts unchanged).
- Messages: "Great!", "Amazing!", "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (twice, around 6/11 and 7/11), "Legendary!".
- Steady play; a few still frames around 3/11 and 6/11.
- About 40 board changes; about 1 min 45 s to solve.

## 4. End of level
- "Flawless!" over a big flower bouquet with a pink bow, confetti; button "Level 28". Coins rose to 3489/3490 during the win.
- No other popup seen.

## 5. Design notes
- Plain sort level: no "?" and no blockers, but 5-long ropes and 11 orders with repeats make it long.
- Differs from B_L027 (other player): different build/HUD (no IQ bar, restart button, boosters visible), and a different board (see B_L027).
- "This rope can't hold that many flowers." is the full-group rule toast.
