# A_L009 (Sort Streak, Level 9)

Source: A_L009 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist). 4 contact sheets, 65 frames (~163 s). All frames looked at.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 9", no difficulty label. Coins 920 -> 1040 at the win.
- No IQ bar (A version UI).
- Orders at start: "0/12".
- No start banner visible.

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes. Pots: white (helper), orange, yellow, red, pink, green.
- Rope length 5.
- Flowers top to bottom:
  1. white: pink, green, pink, green
  2. orange: orange, yellow, red, orange
  3. yellow: green, ?, ?, pink, orange
  4. red: yellow, red, yellow, orange
  5. pink: green, pink, orange
  6. green: red
- 21 flowers, 2 "?". Colours: pink, green, orange, yellow, red (5).
- Free space: 9 empty slots (30 total).

## 3. How it was solved
- The whole first sheet (~50 s, 20 frames) stays at 0/12: lots of shuffling before the first serve.
- Serve order: orange (1/12), red (2/12), pink (3/12), green (4/12), then light blue, yellow, blue (5-7/12), yellow, blue (8-9/12), more to red last (12/12).
- New colours arrive with refill ropes: blue, light blue, lilac, purple.
- Refills arrive full (5 flowers), some with "?". Queue runs out at the end: 6 -> 5 -> 3 ropes -> 2 empty white ropes.
- Boosters: Undo "FREE", Hint 3, Add Rope 1. None used.
- Messages: "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (sheet 2), "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (sheet 3).
- Praise: "Great!", "Incredible!", "Unstoppable!", "Great!", "Amazing!".
- ~150 s of play; moves not countable exactly (~60).

## 4. End of level
- "Excellent!" + bouquet. Next button is RED: "Level 10" with a small flame + "Hard" under it and a coin "x2" badge.
- Coins 920 -> 1040 (+120).

## 5. Design notes
- Same starting board as B_L009.
- Easy in structure (9 free slots, 2 "?", 5 colours) but the player was slow to find the first serve.
- NEW: the win screen announces the next level as Hard (red button, "Hard", coin x2) BEFORE the player starts it.
