# A_L003: Level 3

Source: A_L003 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist). About 2 min 35 s (sheet01 20, sheet02 20, sheet03 13 frames). Frames looked at: start + all 53 sheet frames.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 3". No difficulty label. Coins 140 at start, 208 then 240 at the end.
- IQ bar: none visible.
- Orders: "0/10" (10 grey pot slots).
- No start banner or tip text visible.

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes. Pots: white (helper), red, green, blue, yellow, pink.
- Rope length 4 (up from 3).
- Flowers top to bottom:
  - White: blue, blue, pink, green
  - Red: empty
  - Green: pink, red, green, red
  - Blue: green, yellow, pink (3)
  - Yellow: green, blue, yellow, red
  - Pink: yellow, pink, red (3)
- Colours on board: blue, pink, green, red, yellow (5). Later: light blue, purple, orange, lavender (pale lilac).
- Empty slots: 0 + 4 + 0 + 1 + 0 + 1 = 6.
- No mystery or blockers.

## 3. How it was solved
- Long opening: the counter stays "0/10" for about the first 40 frames (~100 s). The player shuffled flowers between ropes many times before the first serve (lots of moves, some near-identical frames = hesitation).
- Served order: red (1/10), pink ("Nice!", 2/10), green (3/10), blue (4/10, small "Great!"), yellow (5/10), light blue (6/10), purple (7/10), orange + lavender ("Excellent!", 8/10 -> 9/10), red again (10/10).
- **Red came back:** red was the first order and a second red pot arrives later in the queue (red pot at the right end from 3/10); it is the last one served.
- New ropes arrive at the right end holding 4 mixed flowers (full). After the queue empties, white helper ropes slide in (end board: white, red, white).
- Boosters: Undo ("FREE" tag), Hint (light bulb, blue badge "3") newly unlocked, 1 locked tile "Lv.5". No booster use visible (Hint stays 3).
- Praise: "Nice!", "Great!", "Excellent!".
- About 40 to 50 moves, ~2 min 25 s.

## 4. End of level
- Dark overlay "Flawless!", bouquet, button "Level 4". Coins 240.
- No popup.

## 5. Design notes
- First real difficulty step: ropes go from 3 to 4 slots, 5 colours at start, only one empty rope + a full helper rope, so the opening needs planning (the long 0/10 phase).
- New: Hint booster (3 free); a repeated order colour (red twice); lavender next to purple/pink = first look-alike pair.
- Win word changes each level (Excellent, Brilliant, Flawless).
