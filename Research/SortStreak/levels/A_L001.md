# A_L001: Level 1 (tutorial)

Source: A_L001 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist). About 85 s of frames (sheet01 = 20 frames, sheet02 = 4 frames, 2.5 s apart; recording timer in the corner). Frames looked at: start image + all 24 sheet frames.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 1". No difficulty label. Coins 0 during play, 20 when the last pot flies in, 60 on the win screen.
- IQ bar: none visible.
- Orders counter: a pot shelf at the top labelled "0/6" with 6 empty grey pot slots; it fills with coloured pots as they are served (1/6, 2/6, 3/6, 4/6, 6/6).
- Back arrow (top left) and a restart/refresh icon next to it (appears from frame 3).
- Tutorial text boxes, quoted exactly:
  - "Drag the flower stack and drop where the leading flower matches the target's bottom flower."
  - "Match the leading flower to the target's bottom flower. Max 3 flowers per rope."
  - "Any stack fits an empty rope."
  - "Collect the remaining pots!"
- A white pointing hand (glove) shows the drag.

## 2. Starting board
- 4 ropes. Pots above: white (helper), red, blue, yellow.
- Rope length: 3 slots each ("Max 3 flowers per rope.").
- Flowers top to bottom:
  - White/helper: blue (1 flower)
  - Red: red, blue, yellow
  - Blue: red, red
  - Yellow: yellow, blue, yellow
- Colours: red, blue, yellow (3 at first). Later pots: pink, orange, purple (6 pots in total).
- Empty slots at start: 2 + 0 + 1 + 0 = 3.
- During the tutorial, drop targets are highlighted: green rope + green check for a valid target, pink rope + red X for an invalid one.
- No mystery or blockers.

## 3. How it was solved
- Served order: red (0/6 -> 1/6, first pot appears on the shelf), blue ("Nice!", 1/6 -> 2/6), yellow ("Great!", 2/6 -> 3/6), then purple ("Excellent!" small text, 4/6), then pink and orange ("Incredible!", 6/6).
- After the first 3 served, new pots/ropes arrive: pink, orange, purple, already holding flowers (e.g. pink rope: purple, orange, purple; orange rope: purple, orange, orange; purple rope: pink, orange, pink). They arrive part-mixed, not empty.
- When the queue runs out, served ropes leave just the white helper ropes; at the end two white ropes and one orange rope remain, then the orange rope is served.
- Boosters: bottom bar shows 3 locked tiles labelled "Lv.2", "Lv.3", "Lv.5" (padlock icons). None usable.
- Praise: "Nice!", "Great!", "Excellent!", "Incredible!".
- No rejection message seen. No hesitation frames worth noting (tutorial pacing).
- About 12 to 14 moves, ~75 s.

## 4. End of level
- Dark overlay, "Excellent!" title, a big flower bouquet with butterflies, confetti, blue button "Level 2". Coins 60 in the HUD.
- No other popup visible.

## 5. Design notes
- Easy: 3-slot ropes, 3 colours on screen, one helper rope, step-by-step text.
- Teaches the full rule set in one level: match the bottom flower, max per rope, empty rope accepts anything, queue of pots.
- Locked booster tiles with their unlock level ("Lv.2/3/5") are shown from level 1: a promise of what comes.
- Valid/invalid targets shown with green/pink rope tint + check/X during the tutorial: good UX to copy.
