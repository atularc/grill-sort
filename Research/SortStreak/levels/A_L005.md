# A_L005: Level 5 (Hard)

Source: A_L005 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist). About 2 min 25 s (sheet01 20, sheet02 20, sheet03 18 frames). Frames looked at: start + all 58 sheet frames. The video ends on the last serve; no win screen is in the frames.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 5" with a red **"Hard"** label (flame icon) under it. Coins 350 the whole video.
- IQ bar: none visible.
- Orders: "0/12".
- Start banner (frame 1): a red ribbon with a skull/flame badge and "Hard", then a white panel: "Only 50.54% reached the finish. Be one of them!" (the percentage in orange).
- Booster unlock intro (frame 2 / start image): dark overlay, big title "ROPE", a glowing coiled-rope icon, text "Use Rope To Add a Line!". The third booster tile (was locked "Lv.5") is highlighted with a green "FREE" tag.

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes at start (start image): pots white (helper), lavender, blue, yellow, red, green. Right after the ROPE intro a 7th rope with a **white** pot appears at the right end (the free Rope booster was used / given).
- Rope length 5 (up from 4).
- Flowers top to bottom (frame 3, partly approximate):
  - White: green (1)
  - Lavender: lavender (1)
  - Blue: blue, green, yellow, blue, lavender
  - Yellow: yellow, red, blue, yellow, yellow
  - Red: blue, red, green, lavender, red
  - Green: red, yellow, lavender, green, red
  - White (added): empty
- Colours on board: green, lavender, blue, yellow, red (5). Later: orange, pink, light blue, purple.
- Empty slots: 4 + 4 + 0 + 0 + 0 + 0 (+5 on the added rope) = 8 (13 with the extra rope).
- No mystery or blockers.

## 3. How it was solved
- Served order: lavender (1/12), blue (2/12), yellow (3/12), red (4/12), green (5/12), "Amazing!", orange (6/12), "Incredible!", pink (7/12), blue again (8/12, "Unstoppable!"), light blue (9/12), yellow again (10/12, "Legendary!"), purple (11/12), red again (last, "Legendary!", the video ends while it is served).
- Repeats: blue, yellow and red each ordered twice.
- Arrivals: full 5-flower mixed ropes at the right end. Near the end several white helper ropes remain (3 white ropes + red + purple).
- Boosters: Undo ("FREE"), Hint (3), Rope (after the intro its badge shows "1"). No further booster use seen.
- Messages quoted: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (4 times), "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (once).
- Hesitation: long stall at 1/12 (~20 frames, ~50 s) and around 4/12-5/12.
- About 55 to 65 moves, ~2 min 20 s.

## 4. End of level
- Not visible (video ends on the final serve). L4's win screen had already shown "Level 5" with "Hard" and "x2" coins, so the Hard reward is probably double coins (from L4, not from this video).

## 5. Design notes
- First **Hard** level, at level 5: label under the level number, red banner with a pass-rate line ("Only 50.54% reached the finish."), red Next button on the previous win screen, x2 coins.
- Harder through longer ropes (5), 12 orders, three repeated colours, look-alikes (blue / light blue, purple / lavender / pink, red / orange).
- New booster **Rope** ("Use Rope To Add a Line!") is introduced on the Hard level, given free: the game hands the tool right when it is needed.
