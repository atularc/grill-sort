## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order: light blue (first pot flies at 0/11), blue (2/11), purple (3/11, "Great!"), red (4/11, "Incredible!"), ... 6/11, yellow (7/11, "Unstoppable!"), 8/11, red/orange (9/11), blue, 11/11.
- Vine: "2" -> "1" after the first serve, gone after the second serve.
- Most "?" were in the top half of ropes; they turned into real colours as the flowers under them were moved away (green, red, purple, pink seen after reveal).
- New ropes arrive full (pink, yellow, orange, blue, red ropes; some with "?"). From about 6/11 there is a second white helper rope at the right end of the row. When the queue ran out: white, blue, red, orange, white, and at the end only the two white ropes.
- Boosters: Undo "FREE", Hint 3, Add Rope 1. None used.
- Messages: "Great!", "Incredible!", "Unstoppable!", and the rejection bubble "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (at 8/11).
- Hesitation: a few same-looking frames at 2/11 and 8/11.
- Rough moves: ~30 board changes, ~105 s.

## 5. Design notes
- The challenge is information, not space: 8 "?" at the start (whole top rows hidden), but 8 free slots incl. an empty purple rope, so it plays fast.
- The second white helper rope shows up mid-level again (also L22) - looks like a fixed part of the late queue.
