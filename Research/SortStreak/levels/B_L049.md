# B_L049

Source: B_L049 (B playlist). 4 contact sheets (20 + 20 + 20 + 2 frames, ~155 s). Looked at: start image + sheets 01-04.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 49", no difficulty label. Coins 706 at start, 716 then 717 after the win.
- IQ bar 40 / 90 / 180; fills slowly, no "IQ GENIUS!" screen seen.
- Orders "0/11".
- No start banner visible (no streak banner, consistent with the streak lost on L48).
- Booster bar: not visible (cropped).

- 6 ropes, pots: white (helper), light blue, green, blue, red, orange.
- Rope length: 5 slots.
- Flowers top to bottom:
  - white: green, red, orange
  - light blue: empty
  - green: light blue, green, blue, ?, red
  - blue: ?, blue, ?, green, red

  - orange: orange, orange, light blue, ?, green

## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order (shelf): orange, red, light blue, green, blue ("Amazing!" around 4-5/11), lilac, pink, yellow, red, blue, purple ... 11 total. Lilac, pink, yellow, purple come from the queue.
- First move: light blue from the bottom of a rope onto the empty light-blue rope, dragging a "?" with it.
- New ropes arrive full from the right, often with 1-2 "?". Queue end: white pots, 2 white ropes at 11/11.
- Toasts (exact): "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (twice), "Match the bottom flower to drop here!"
- Praise: "Amazing!", "Legendary x2", "Legendary".
- Boosters: not visible, none used. No resets.
- Rough move count ~45-55; ~2 min.

## 4. End of level
- Coins 706 -> 717.
- "Leaderboard": own score 362 (top).
- "Fantastic!" flower cart; a stats box "Time 0..." partly hidden behind a large pink/white **"S" grade badge** stamping in (first time a letter grade is seen in my batch).
- Fade to a green patch (cart transition).

## 5. Design notes
- Normal level, many "?" (6) but only 5 starting colours and one fully empty rope.
- Mid-game palette lilac / pink / purple are look-alikes and slow the player down.
- Win screen may show a letter grade ("S") over the stats.
