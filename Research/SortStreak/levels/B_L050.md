# B_L050

Source: B_L050 (B playlist). 4 contact sheets (20 + 20 + 20 + 5 frames, ~160 s). Looked at: start image + sheets 01-04.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 50", label flame + "Hard". Coins 717 at start, 727 after the win (+10).
- IQ bar 40 / 90 / 180; "IQ GENIUS!" screen at the end.
- Orders "0/10".
- Start popups (in this order): "Hard" ribbon with "Only 52.44% reached the finish. Be one of them!", then trophy "2 Streak!" / "Petal power!".
- Booster bar: not visible (cropped).

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes, pots: white (helper), light blue, orange, red, green, blue.
- **Rope length: 6 slots** (two ropes hold 6 flowers) - longer than the 5 seen in L44-L49.
- Flowers top to bottom:
  - white: green, orange, green, blue, light blue
  - light blue: light blue, red, green, ?, orange, blue
  - orange: ?, orange, ?, light blue
  - red: red
  - green: orange, green, light blue, ?, light blue
  - blue: blue, ?, blue, orange, red, blue
- Colours: green, orange, blue, light blue, red (5).
- Empty slots: 9 (white 1, orange 2, red 5, green 1).
- Specials: 5 "?". No vines.

## 3. How it was solved
- **Long opening with no serve**: the whole of sheet 01 plus 2 frames of sheet 02 (~55 s) is rearranging before the first pot is served - the player builds up several colours at once.
- Serve order (shelf): light blue, blue, orange, red ("Great!"), green ("Excellent!"), ... then yellow ("Amazing!"), purple, pink, blue, purple/pink ... 10 total. Yellow, purple, pink from the queue.
- Once set up, serves come in quick chains.
- New ropes arrive full (6) from the right with some "?". Queue end: white pots, 2 white ropes at 10/10.
- Praise: "Great!", "Excellent!", "Amazing!", "Great!", "Excellent!".
- No rejection toast seen. Boosters: not visible, none used.
- Time 02:04 (win screen); steps not readable.

## 4. End of level
- "IQ GENIUS!" sunflower screen.
- "Leaderboard": own score 362 -> 372 (top).
- "Awesome!" flower cart; stats "Time 02:04" with a red header and a small badge, a second box greyed out (not readable).
- Coins 717 -> 727.

## 5. Design notes
- Hard level at L50 (L45 Hard, L50 Hard: every ~5 levels in this stretch).
- Hardness here comes from longer ropes (6), only 9 free slots and 5 "?": the first serve needs a long plan.
- Level 50 had no special celebration/milestone popup (not visible).
