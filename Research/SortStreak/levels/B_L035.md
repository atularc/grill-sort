# B_L035 (Level 35)

Source: B_L035 (B = Sort Streak Walkthrough playlist). 3 sheets, about 58 frames x 2.5 s = about 2 min 25 s. Frames looked at: start image, sheet01-03 (all).

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 35" with red flame icon + **"Hard"** under it. Coins 482 at start, 492 at the end.
- IQ bar 40 / 90 / IQ badge / 180. Ends at about 90 (badge turns red at 10/10).
- Orders "0/10".
- Banners at start, in this order:
  1. Red ribbon with a flower diamond icon, "Hard", and "50.97% fail here. Try your best!"
  2. Trophy: "3 Streak!" / "What a streak!"

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes. Pots: white (helper), green, red, lilac, blue, purple.
- Rope length: **6 slots** (longer than L33/L34's 5).
- Flowers top to bottom:
  1. white: lilac, ?, green, blue, green
  2. green: green, purple
  3. red: lilac, ?, purple, green, blue
  4. lilac: red, lilac, red, lilac, ?, blue
  5. blue: blue, ?, red, ?, purple
  6. purple: purple, ?, lilac, ?, blue
- 28 pieces in 36 slots = 8 empty. 7 "?" at the start. 6 colours at the start (green, purple, lilac, red, blue + white helper); orange, yellow, sunflower-yellow (brown centre), pink, light blue arrive later.
- No vines / locks / coins.

## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order: green (1/10, "Nice!"), blue, red, purple, lilac (5/10), then sunflower (6/10), light blue, orange, red... final shelf: green, purple, blue, red, lilac, sunflower, light blue, red, orange, pink (red twice).
- New ropes arrive full (6 pieces) with 1-3 "?" each. Second white helper appears on the right near the end; at 10/10 only 2 white ropes remain.
- Boosters: no booster bar visible.
- Messages: praise "Nice!", "Amazing!", "Great!", "Amazing!" (orange-red). No rejection text seen.
- Hesitation: long stall at 5/10 - sheet02 has 20 frames (about 50 s) at 5/10 with slow rearranging (orange, yellow, pink, red, light blue all mixed, lots of "?"). This is the hard middle.
- Moves: about 45 board changes; about 2 min.

## 4. End of level
- "IQ GENIUS!" sunflower popup.
- Coins fly into the pill: 482 -> 483 ... -> 492 (+10).
- "Leaderboard" (timer about 36:31; podium 140 / 181 / 131; rows "VozDoMar_01 128 Playing now", "HeartSeeker 87", "VagueMediterra... 77", "ZlotyWilk 67").
- "Brilliant!" flower cart; now with three stat boxes **"Time" / "Steps" / "... Streak"** (win streak) and text (partly blurred) "Minimalist Master! Your step count ...shed 99% of opponents!".

## 5. Design notes
- Hard because: rope length grows to 6, 7 mystery pieces at start, only 8 free slots, look-alike colours (red/orange/pink/yellow/sunflower, blue/light blue, purple/lilac).
- New: first "Hard" label in this set, with a fail-rate banner ("50.97% fail here. Try your best!"); results screen shows Time / Steps / Streak and a percentile line about step count.
- Hard level is a sawtooth peak right after two normal levels (L33, L34).
