## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order (counter): yellow (1/12), orange (2/12), red (3/12), blue (4/12), green (5/12, "Amazing!"), ... 7/12, 8/12, blue (9/12, "Great!"), pink, ... 12/12. Yellow came back (the second yellow pot), blue came back, red came back later as a red pot in the late row.
- After each serve the served rope leaves, ropes shift, and a new rope with a pot arrives at the right, already holding flowers (e.g. lilac pot rope with lilac/blue flowers, light-blue pot). Some new ropes carry "?" flowers. When the queue ran out, the row shrank; at the end only the two white helper ropes remained (frame before the win screen).
- Vine: the disc counted down "2" -> "1" after the first serve (yellow), and the vine burst (orange splash) after the second serve (orange); then the blue rope's flowers were free. So the vine opens after N serves anywhere on the board.
- Boosters: Undo shown as "FREE" (green tag), Hint (bulb) 3, Add Rope (rope coil) 1. None of them seen used.
- Messages: "Amazing!", "Great!", and one rejection bubble: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (at 8/12).
- Hesitation: several near-identical frames at 2/12 and at 8/12 (thinking). No undo seen.
- Rough moves: about 30 board changes; solved in about 80 s.

## 5. Design notes
- Hard part: the vine locks a whole full rope (4 flowers) for the first 2 serves, so the start plays with 6 ropes; plus 3 "?" flowers and only 1 helper rope.
- NEW vs. earlier (as far as this video shows): vine blocker with a serve counter disc.
- UI: shelf at the top fills with the served pots, one per order; the vine counter is a large brown disc in the middle of the rope - very readable.
