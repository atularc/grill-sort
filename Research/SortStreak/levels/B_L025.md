# B_L025

Source: B_L025 (B = Sort Streak Walkthrough playlist), video 188 s, frames looked at: `B_L025_start.png`, `B_L025_sheet01.png` (20), `_sheet02.png` (20), `_sheet03.png` (20), `_sheet04.png` (15).

**Hard in both builds, but a different board from A_L025** (A: 0/10, blue/light-blue/yellow/red/green; B: 0/11, pink/red + lilac/purple look-alikes) and a different banner text.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 25" with flame icon + **"Hard"** under it. Coins "309.." (last digit hidden).
- IQ bar "40 / 90 / IQ / 180": empty at start, about half at 6/11, IQ icon red at 11/11 (milestone reached).
- Orders "0/11".
- Start banners, in order: (1) red ribbon card with flower badge **"Hard"** and **"Only 45.69% reached the finish. Be one of them!"** (45.69% in orange); (2) trophy banner **"2 Streak!"** / **"One win leads to another!"**.

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes. Pots: white (helper), blue, pink, red, lilac, purple.
- Rope length 6.
- Flowers, top to bottom (from the first clear frame; the first frame shows ropes swinging in and the banner covers the middle, so rope 1 may already hold one move):
  1. white: red, lilac, blue
  2. blue: ?, pink, red, ?, blue
  3. pink: ?, red, pink, purple, blue
  4. red: pink
  5. lilac: purple, pink, ?, blue, purple, blue
  6. purple: pink, red, lilac, red, ?, lilac
- Colours: blue, pink, red, lilac, purple (5) - two look-alike pairs (pink/red, lilac/purple). Orange, light blue, green, yellow arrive later.
- Free space: about 10 empty slots.
- Blockers: 5 "?" flowers. No vine.

## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order: blue (1/11, "Nice!"), purple (2/11), red (3/11), lilac + pink (5/11, "Excellent!"), blue (6/11), ... red (7/11, "Great!"), ... 9/11, 11/11.
- New ropes arrive full of 6 with "?" (green, orange, light blue, yellow ropes). From 6/11 a second white helper rope sits at the right end. End: two white ropes.
- Boosters: not visible (bottom cut off).
- Messages: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (twice at 6/11), "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (at 7/11), praise "Nice!", "Excellent!", "Great!".
- Hesitation: stuck around 6/11 for ~20 frames (~50 s) with two rejections - the orange/green/light-blue/yellow phase was the hard part.
- Rough moves: win screen "Steps 77", "Time 02:33".

## 4. End of level
- Coins fly to the pill: 309x -> 312x -> 320x.
- "IQ GENIUS!" splash.
- Leaderboard: "sort" 17th (50 flowers) -> 15th -> **4th (61 flowers)** - a big jump (Hard levels seem to give more leaderboard flowers: +11).
- Win screen **"Outstanding!"** (orange letters) with the flower cart; on this Hard level the stat tiles have **red** headers: "Time 02:33", "Steps 77" (both with an "S" grade badge), "Win Streak" grey "1".
- No other popup.

## 5. Design notes
- Hard here = 6 ropes x 6 slots, two look-alike pairs at the start, 5 "?", 11 orders. A's Hard board was different (10 orders, blue/light-blue) - the builds use different level data for the same number.
- The Hard banner text changes between builds/players: "53.18% could not do it, the rest are legends" (A) vs "Only 45.69% reached the finish. Be one of them!" (B) - a social-proof line with a percentage.
- Hard win screen styling: orange title and red stat tiles; A's Hard win used "Perfect!" in orange too.
