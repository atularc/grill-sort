## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order (from the shelf icons): purple, green, yellow, pink, blue, red, orange, ... (later pots: yellow, red, blue, purple, orange, light blue, red, blue). New colours (pink, orange, light blue, red again) appear only via the queue. Some colours are served twice (red, blue, yellow).
- After a serve the pot leaves, pots shift, and a new rope arrives full on the right (5 flowers, often with "?" in it). Late in the level the queue runs out: the served positions are replaced by white pots (sheet 03 shows white pots at both ends), then only 2 white ropes remain at 12/12.
- "?" flowers turned into colours once they became the bottom flower.
- Boosters: not visible; no booster use seen.
- Praise texts seen: "Nice!", "Great!", "Amazing!", "Incredible!", "Unstoppable!", "Legendary x4" (combo-style escalating words on consecutive serves).
- No rejection messages seen. A few stretches with little change (player thinking) mid sheet 02.
- Roughly 40-50 moves; ~2 min to solve.

## 4. End of level
- Board empties to two white ropes, "12/12".
- "Leaderboard" screen with a timer, top 3 podium (animal avatars with gift boxes and flower scores, e.g. 295 / 269 / 260) and a list of players ("Playing now" / "x min ago" tags).
- Then "Superb!" with a flower cart (shelf of potted flowers on wheels) being filled: a collection/decoration screen.
- Coins 645 -> 657.
- No other popups visible.

## 5. Design notes
- Tight start: 9 free slots for 5 colours with 4 mystery flowers; 12 orders through a queue keeps the board full.
- Full arrivals with "?" inside keep uncertainty going all level.
- Escalating praise chain ("Nice!" -> "Legendary x4") rewards fast consecutive serves.
- Leaderboard + flower cart collection after the win are meta layers worth noting.
