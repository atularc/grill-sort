# B_L026

Source: B_L026 (B = Sort Streak Walkthrough playlist), video 117 s, frames looked at: `B_L026_start.png`, `B_L026_sheet01.png` (20), `_sheet02.png` (20), `_sheet03.png` (7).

A_L026 only shows the end (from 8/12), so the boards can't be fully compared; both are 12 orders and A's end phase (light blue / orange / purple / yellow, two white helpers) looks like B's end phase.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 26", no difficulty label. Coins "320.." -> "332.." (last digit hidden by overlay).
- IQ bar "40 / 90 / IQ / 180": empty at start, ~1/4 at 3/12, IQ icon red at 12/12.
- Orders "0/12".
- Before the level: Home screen transition (hexagon scene picture, snowy gingerbread house = the "Scene" puzzle).
- Start banner: trophy **"3 Streak!"** / **"Petal power!"**.

## 2. Starting board
- 7 ropes. Pots: white (helper), green, lilac, blue, yellow, red, pink.
- Rope length 4.
- Flowers, top to bottom:
  1. white: lilac, pink, ?, pink
  2. green: green
  3. lilac: clover VINE with a counter disc **"4"** (hidden, looks full)
  4. blue: clover VINE with a **sign disc showing a green flower icon** (hidden, looks full)
  5. yellow: ?, ?, red
  6. red: red, green
  7. pink: pink, green
- Colours: lilac, pink, green, red (+ blue/yellow under vines). Light blue, orange, purple come later.
- Free space: about 8 empty slots.
- Blockers: 3 "?" flowers; **two kinds of vine**: a count vine "4" (opens after 4 serves) and a **goal vine** whose disc shows a green flower (opens when the green order is served).

## 3. How it was solved
- Early on the player tapped the goal vine and got: **"This rope is locked. Complete the goal on the sign first!"**
- Serve order: green (1/12, first serve: the green-sign vine on the blue rope opened at once, and the count vine went 4 -> 3), pink (2/12, vine "2"), blue (3/12, vine "1"), red (4/12, vine opened, "Amazing!"), red/blue (5/12, "Incredible!"), 6/12 "Unstoppable!", lilac (7/12, "Legendary!"), ... 9/12 "Legendary x2", ... 12/12 "Legendary x5".
- New ropes arrive full, some with "?" (light blue, orange, purple ropes). Second white helper rope seen from ~8/12. End: two white ropes.
- Boosters: not visible (bottom cut off).
- Messages: "This rope is locked. Complete the goal on the sign first!", "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (twice, at 0/12 and 1/12), praise "Good Luck!", "Great!", "Amazing!", "Incredible!", "Unstoppable!", "Legendary!", "Legendary x2", "Legendary x5".
- Rough moves: win screen "Steps 48", "Time 01:14".

## 4. End of level
- "IQ GENIUS!" splash.
- Leaderboard: "sort" 5th (63 flowers) -> **4th (73 flowers)**, toast "Give it your all and make the next round even better!".
- Win screen "Superb!" flower cart, tiles "Time 01:14", "Steps 48", "Win Streak 4" (blue tiles, normal level), line **"Blazing Speed! Your time was faster than 99% of players!"**.
- Coins 320x -> 332x.

## 5. Design notes
- NEW: **goal vine** (sign shows a flower colour; opens when that colour's order is served) next to the count vine. Tapping a locked rope gives an explaining toast.
- Two vines with different rules on one board = more planning (serve green first to free the blue rope).
- Combo praise climbs to "Legendary x5" for a long serve chain.
- Streak banner text changes each time ("Keep that winning rhythm!", "One win leads to another!", "Petal power!").
