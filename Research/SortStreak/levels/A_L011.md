# A_L011 (Sort Streak, Level 11)

Source: A_L011 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist). 3 contact sheets, 45 frames (~113 s). All frames looked at.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 11", no difficulty label. Coins 1280 -> 1380.
- No IQ bar (A version).
- Orders at start: "0/10".
- Tutorial overlay on a dimmed board (first 2 frames): "Locked Flowers" / "Fill a pot of the marked color to unlock these flowers." / "Tap to Continue". Only the locked rope is lit.

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes. Pots: white (helper), red, pink, lilac, yellow, orange.
- Rope length 5.
- Flowers top to bottom:
  1. white: yellow, orange, red, orange, red
  2. red: red
  3. pink: yellow, pink, lilac, pink, lilac
  4. lilac: lilac, pink, yellow, red
  5. yellow: LOCKED: covered by a green clover vine with small white flowers over the whole rope; a round wooden badge with a RED flower on it in the middle.
  6. orange: orange, yellow, lilac, orange, red
- Visible 20 flowers, no "?" visible at the start (the locked rope's content is hidden). Colours: yellow, orange, red, pink, lilac.
- Free space on open ropes: 5 slots.

## 3. How it was solved
- Red served first (1/10, ~12 s): the vine disappears (unlock condition = serve a red pot).
- After the unlock the board shows ropes with stacks of "?" (3 in a row) on the right.
- Serve order: red (1/10), orange (2/10), lilac (3/10), yellow (4/10), pink (5/10), red (6/10, second red order), light blue (7/10), purple, blue, green (10/10).
- Refills full, some with "?". End: 2 empty white ropes.
- Boosters: Undo "FREE", Hint 3, Add Rope 1. Not used.
- Messages: "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (sheet 2).
- Praise: "Nice!", "Great!", "Excellent!", "Amazing!", "Incredible!".

## 4. End of level
- "Unstoppable!" + bouquet, "Level 12" button. Coins 1280 -> 1380 (+100).

## 5. Design notes
- NEW MECHANIC: Locked Flowers (vine lock) at Level 11, with a one-screen tutorial. The badge colour = the pot colour you must serve to unlock.
- In A the lock sits on a customer rope (yellow pot) and needs RED; B_L011 has a different board (lock on the white helper rope, needs PURPLE).
- The unlock colour (red) is served almost immediately: a gentle first use.
- Red is ordered twice in this level.
