- New ropes arrive full (5) from the right, many with 1-2 "?" flowers; late arrivals have several "?" (e.g. ropes with ?, ?, ? stacked). When the queue runs out the slots become white pots and ropes empty out.

## 5. Design notes
- First "Hard" level in this batch (L45), right after a normal L44: sawtooth pacing. Pre-level popup gives a social completion % ("Only 49.59% reached the finish").
- First vine blocker seen in my batch: covers a whole rope and opens when a pot of the colour on its badge is served.
- Many "?" in the late queue ropes keep the end hard.
- Win screen shows Time / Steps / Win Streak plus a percentile brag line: nice cheap motivator.
