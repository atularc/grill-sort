# A_L030

Source: A_L030 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist). 57 sheet frames (sheet01 20, sheet02 20, sheet03 17) at 2.5 s = about 2 min 20 s of video. Frames looked at: `A_L030_start.png`, all 3 sheets.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 30" with a red flame icon and **"Hard"** under the level number (first Hard level in this set). Coins 3720 at start, 3919 after the last serve, 3920 on the win screen (+200; the previous win screen promised "x2" coins).
- No IQ bar (A's build).
- Orders "0/10".
- No streak banner seen at the start.

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes; pots: white (helper), red, lilac, light blue, purple, orange.
- Rope length 6 (the lilac rope holds 6; others hold 5 or fewer at the start).
- Flowers (top to bottom):
  - white: purple, lilac, light blue, ?, orange
  - red: red, lilac, ?, purple, red
  - lilac: light blue, ?, light blue, orange, red, orange (full)
  - light blue: purple, lilac, lilac, ?, orange
  - purple: purple, lilac
  - orange: orange, ?, orange, red
- 27 flowers in 36 slots = 9 empty. **5 mystery "?"** at the start. Colours at start: purple, lilac, light blue, orange, red.
- No vines or other blockers.

## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order (shelf): orange, red, light blue, lilac, purple, blue, pink, then green, yellow, red to finish (10).
- Arriving ropes come full (6 pieces), several with 1-3 "?" (green, yellow, blue, pink ropes). Queue end: a second white helper appeared; the last ropes were finished next to 2 white ropes.
- Boosters: Undo "FREE", Hint 3, Add Rope 1. None used.
- Messages: "Excellent!", "This rope can't hold that many flowers.", "Legendary!" (twice).
- **Very slow opening:** 21 frames (about 52 s) at 0/10 with many small moves before the first serve (orange). After that serves came steadily.
- About 2 min 15 s to solve, around 50+ board changes.

## 4. End of level
- "Flawless!" bouquet, button "Level 31" (blue, no Hard tag). Coins 3920 (doubled reward).
- No other popup.

## 5. Design notes
- Hard = longer ropes (6), only 9 free slots, 5 hidden pieces at the start, fewer orders (10) and no vines. The difficulty is in the opening; it gets easier once the first pot is served.
- The Hard tag is shown both on the previous win screen's button ("Level 30 / Hard / x2") and under the level number in the HUD.
- Hard levels pay 2x coins (≈200 vs ≈110 on normal levels for this player).
