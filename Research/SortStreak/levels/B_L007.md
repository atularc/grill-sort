# B_L007 (Sort Streak, Level 7)

Source: B_L007 (B = Sort Streak Walkthrough playlist). 4 contact sheets, 70 frames (~175 s of video). All frames looked at.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 7", no difficulty label. Coins 530 during play, 64x after the win (last digits cut off by the video's facecam overlay).
- IQ bar: yes, milestones 40 / 90 / 180 (IQ badge between 90 and 180). Fills slowly; near the end it is past 90, under 180 (no "IQ GENIUS" popup in this level).
- Orders counter at start: "0/11".
- Before the level: Home screen "Scene 1" hexagon picture puzzle "6/25", locked avatar "Lv.20", big "Level 7" button.
- Level start popup: gold trophy with flowers, "6 Streak!" / "A new high for you!".

## 2. Starting board
- 7 ropes. Pots left to right: white (helper), green, light blue, blue, purple, red, pink.
- Rope length 5 slots (all ropes).
- Flowers top to bottom (ropes sway in on entry):
  1. white: ?, blue, ?, purple, pink
  2. green: green, ?, light blue, green, green
  3. light blue: ?, purple, red, light blue, green
  4. blue: purple
  5. purple: purple, ?, pink, ?, green
  6. red: (empty)
  7. pink: ?, blue, ?, light blue, green
- 26 flowers, 8 of them "?" (grey mystery). Visible colours: green, light blue, blue, purple, red, pink (6).
- Free space: 9 empty slots (35 slots total).
- Blockers: only mystery "?" flowers.

## 3. How it was solved
- First serve: green (1/11) after ~70 s of play. Then red (2/11), purple (3/11), then pink/blue/light blue and others; later pots seen in the queue: orange, yellow, lilac (new colours arrive with new ropes). Exact order after 4/11 not reliably readable.
- New ropes after a serve slide in from the right and arrive full (5 flowers), several with "?" flowers in them.
- Near the end the queue runs out: rope count shrinks to 6, 5, 4, 3, then 2 empty white ropes.
- Boosters: not visible (bottom of the screen is cropped in this video).
- Messages: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (sheet 1), "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (sheet 2).
- Praise: "Nice!", "Excellent!", "Amazing!", "Legendary X2".
- Long hesitation at the start: board barely changes for ~10 frames (~25 s) on sheet 1, and again before the first serve.
- Results screen: Time 02:19, Steps 61.

## 4. End of level
- Win screen: dark background, "On fire!", a flower cart fills with pots one by one. Stat tiles: "Time 02:19", "Steps 61", "Win Streak 7". Text: "Lightning Fast! Your time outperformed 99% of players!".
- Coins 530 -> 64x (about +110/+120).
- No other popups visible.

## 5. Design notes
- Hardest early factor: 8 mystery flowers out of 26 and only one empty rope (red) plus the helper; a long slow opening.
- Look-alike colours: blue vs light blue, purple vs pink/lilac.
- 11 orders with 7 ropes means a queue of ~5 refill ropes.
- UI worth copying: streak trophy popup at level start, results cart with Time / Steps / Win Streak and a "% of players" brag line; praise words escalate during a run of quick serves (Nice -> Excellent -> Amazing -> Legendary X2).
