# A_L025

Source: A_L025 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist), video 158 s, frames looked at: `A_L025_start.png`, `A_L025_sheet01.png` (20), `_sheet02.png` (20), `_sheet03.png` (20), `_sheet04.png` (3).

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 25" with a red flame icon and **"Hard"** under it (stays all level). Coins 3060.
- IQ bar: not visible (A build).
- Orders at start "0/10".
- Start banner: a red ribbon card with a flower badge, **"Hard"**, and text **"53.18% could not do it, the rest are legends"** (53.18% in orange). Shown over the board for the first frame.

## 2. Starting board
- **6 ropes** (one fewer than L21-24). Pots: white (helper), blue, light blue, yellow, red, green.
- **Rope length 6** (longer than the 4-5 of earlier levels).
- Flowers, top to bottom:
  1. white: empty
  2. blue: ?, blue, yellow, blue
  3. light blue: green, green, light blue, red
  4. yellow: yellow, ?, blue, red, green
  5. red: light blue, red, green, ?, green, yellow
  6. green: light blue, ?, ?, yellow, red, blue
- Colours: blue, light blue, yellow, red, green (5, with blue / light blue look-alikes). Orange, purple, pink arrive later.
- Free space: 11 empty slots (one fully empty helper rope of 6).
- Blockers: 5 "?" flowers. No vine.

## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order: blue (1/10), yellow (2/10), red (3/10, "Great!"), light blue (4/10), green (5/10, "Amazing!"), orange (6/10), pink (7/10), purple (8/10, "Legendary!"), blue (9/10), red (10/10, "Legendary!").
- New ropes arrive full of 6, several with 2-3 "?" (orange, purple, pink ropes). From 6/10 a second white helper rope sits at the right end of the row. End: two white ropes.
- At 10/10 a stamp "Hard x2" pops next to the level title.
- Boosters: Undo "FREE", Hint 3, Add Rope 1. None used.
- Messages: "Mystery flowers can only go on empty ropes." (at 6/10), "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (at 7/10), praise "Great!", "Amazing!", "Legendary!".
- Hesitation: long thinking at 0/10 (first ~10 frames, ~25 s) and at 6/10.
- Rough moves: ~40 board changes, ~150 s.

## 4. End of level
- Coins 3060 -> 3260 (**+200**, about double the normal ~+100-120: the "x2" Hard reward).
- Win: "Perfect!" title (yellow/orange letters, not the white of earlier titles), bouquet, button "Level 26" (blue, no Hard tag).
- No other popups.

## 5. Design notes
- FIRST HARD LEVEL in this set. Hard here = fewer, longer ropes (6 ropes x 6 slots), 5 colours incl. blue/light blue, 5 "?", but no vine and a fully empty helper. Big ropes make each order bigger (6 pieces).
- Hard flow: next button on the previous win screen is red with "Hard" + "x2"; level start banner with the fail percentage; "Hard" under the level title; "Hard x2" stamp on completion; double coins.
- Easy-again level follows (L26 button is blue) = sawtooth.
