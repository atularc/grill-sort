# A_L002: Level 2

Source: A_L002 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist). About 85 s (sheet01 = 20 frames, sheet02 = 14 frames). Frames looked at: start + all 34 sheet frames.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 2". No difficulty label. Coins 60 at start, 108 then 140 at the end.
- IQ bar: none visible.
- Orders: "0/8" with 8 empty grey pot slots on the shelf.
- No start banner. Mid-level tip box: "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (after a wrong drop attempt).

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes. Pots: white (helper), blue, purple, pink, orange, green.
- Rope length 3.
- Flowers top to bottom:
  - White: orange
  - Blue: blue, orange, green
  - Purple: empty
  - Pink: purple, pink, green
  - Orange: purple, pink, blue
  - Green: green, blue
- Colours on board: orange, blue, green, purple, pink (5). Later: yellow, light blue (cyan), red.
- Empty slots: 2 + 0 + 3 + 0 + 0 + 1 = 6.
- No mystery or blockers.

## 3. How it was solved
- Served order: green (1/8, pot flies up to the shelf), blue (2/8), pink ("Great!", 3/8), light blue (4/8), orange + purple ("Amazing!", then "Unstoppable!" at 6/8), yellow ("Legendary!", 7/8), red (8/8).
- New ropes arrive at the right end after each serve, already holding 3 mixed flowers (e.g. yellow pot rope: blue, red, yellow; light blue rope: blue, red, ...; red rope: yellow, blue, red, ...). Arrivals look full (3/3).
- When the queue is empty a new white helper pot/rope appears at the right (frame at 3/8 -> 4/8: a white pot slides in). At the end 2 white ropes + the red rope remain.
- Boosters: bottom bar: Undo tile now unlocked with a green "FREE" tag; 2 locked tiles "Lv.3", "Lv.5". Undo was not visibly used (stays "FREE").
- Valid targets glow green with a check while dragging.
- Praise: "Great!", "Amazing!", "Unstoppable!", "Legendary!". Praise escalates with consecutive serves.
- Hesitation: several frames at 3/8 with small changes (a wrong drop gave the tip "Match the bottom flower to drop here!").
- About 18 to 20 moves, ~75 s.

## 4. End of level
- Dark overlay, "Brilliant!", bouquet, "Level 3" button. Coins 140 (coins flew into the counter: 60 -> 108 -> 140).
- No popup.

## 5. Design notes
- Still easy: 3-slot ropes, 1 helper + 1 empty rope at start, 8 orders.
- New: Undo booster unlocked at level 2 with a "FREE" tag; error tip text; extra white helper rope added when the queue runs dry; escalating praise words.
- Queue arrivals are full ropes, so free space comes only from served ropes leaving.
