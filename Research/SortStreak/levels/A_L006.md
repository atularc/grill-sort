# A_L006: Level 6 (mystery flowers introduced)

Source: A_L006 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist). About 2 min (sheet01 20, sheet02 20, sheet03 8 frames). Frames looked at: start + all 48 sheet frames.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 6". No difficulty label. Coins 590 at start (350 at L5 start, so the Hard L5 win paid about +240), 690 on the win screen.
- IQ bar: none visible.
- Orders: "0/10".
- Start banner (frame 1): wooden sign with a pink flower badge "2" and the text "2 Streak! You're on a roll!" (win streak after L5).
- Tutorial text (frame 2 / start image): "Move the flowers below the mystery flower to reveal its color!" with the pointing hand on a green flower.

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes. Pots: white (helper), green, pink, blue, lavender, red.
- Rope length 5.
- Flowers top to bottom:
  - White: lavender, blue, pink, green
  - Green: green
  - Pink: blue, ?, red, ?, red
  - Blue: ?, ?, blue, green
  - Lavender: ?, lavender
  - Red: blue, ?, ?, green
- "?" = grey flower with a white question mark. 7 mystery flowers at start.
- Colours visible: lavender, blue, pink, green, red (5); later orange, light blue, purple, yellow.
- Empty slots: 1 + 4 + 0 + 1 + 3 + 1 = 10.

## 3. How it was solved
- Mystery reveals: a "?" turns into its real colour with a small burst when it becomes the bottom flower (e.g. on the lavender rope the "?" became red; on the blue rope "?"s became blue/red).
- Served order: green (1/10), lavender (2/10, "Nice!"), red (3/10), blue (4/10, "Excellent!"), orange (5/10), pink (6/10), red again (7/10), light blue ("Nice!"), purple, yellow (9/10 -> 10/10, "Excellent!").
- Repeat: red ordered twice.
- Arrivals: full 5-flower ropes, and **queued ropes also contain "?" flowers** (orange rope and the later light-blue / purple / yellow ropes carried 1-2 "?" each).
- After the queue empties, white helper ropes fill in (end: 2 white ropes).
- Boosters: Undo ("FREE"), Hint (3), Rope (1). None used.
- Messages: "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (once, at 6/10).
- Hesitation: ~12 frames at 0/10 and ~10 at 6/10.
- About 40 moves, ~1 min 50 s.

## 4. End of level
- "Impressive!", bouquet, confetti, blue button "Level 7". Coins 690 (+100).
- No popup.

## 5. Design notes
- New mechanic at level 6: **mystery "?" flowers**, taught with one text line + hand; 7 on the start board and more in queued ropes.
- New UI: **win streak banner** at level start ("2 Streak! You're on a roll!").
- After the Hard L5 this is a relief level: plenty of free space (10 empty slots), only 10 orders, no Hard label. Sawtooth pacing (Hard, then easier with a new mechanic).
