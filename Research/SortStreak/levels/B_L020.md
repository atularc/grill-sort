# B_L020

Source: B_L020 (B = Sort Streak Walkthrough playlist), video 221 s, frames looked at: start image + sheet01 (20), sheet02 (20), sheet03 (20), sheet04 (20), sheet05 (8).

NOTE: newer build (IQ bar, pink background). Unlike L17-L19, the starting board is the SAME as A_L020 (same 6 ropes, same flowers, same 0/10). Booster bar cropped out.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 20" with red "Hard" label (flame icon). Coins "223x" at start (cropped), "233x" after the win (~+100; the A build gave +200 for this level; exact B numbers cut off by the overlay).
- IQ bar: "40", "90", IQ badge, "180"; fills a step per serve, ends around the 40-90 mark.
- Orders counter: "0/10".
- Banner at start: Hard intro popup, red ribbon "Hard" with the flower diamond icon, text: "Only 58.81% reached the finish. Be one of them!" (A build: "Only 53.99% pass this level. Can you do it?" — wording and % differ). No streak banner (this player's streak is 1).

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes. Pots: white (helper), green, pink, red, blue, yellow.
- Rope length: 6 slots.
- Flowers, top to bottom (identical to A_L020):
  - helper: blue, pink, yellow, green, yellow
  - green: green, red, yellow, blue, red, green
  - pink: blue, red, pink, blue, pink, red
  - red: blue, pink, yellow, green
  - blue: red, pink
  - yellow: yellow, green, yellow, green
- 5 colours, no "?", no vines. Free space 9 of 36.

## 3. How it was solved
- 0/10 for about 35 s (sheet01), then: green (1/10), yellow (2/10), blue (3/10), pink (4/10), red (5/10), then a very long stall at 5/10 (~95 s, sheets 02 bottom row to 04 mid) with red, orange, purple, lavender and light blue ropes (look-alike pairs red/orange, purple/lavender, blue/light blue) and 2 ropes nearly empty; then orange (6/10), red/light blue (7/10), purple, lavender to 10/10.
- Serve order differs from A (A: green, yellow, red, pink, blue...; B: green, yellow, blue, pink, red...).
- New ropes arrive full (6 pieces), no "?" seen. Row shrank to 4, then 2.
- Rejection: "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (sheet04 frame 2).
- Praise: "Nice!", "Great!", "Amazing!".
- About 70+ board changes, ~205 s. Win screen stats not captured in the frames.

## 4. End of level
- "Perfect!" with the flower cart (no IQ GENIUS splash seen; stats panel not in frames).
- Home screen: "Scene 1" jigsaw "19/25", then the "20/25" step; a timer badge top-left (sunflower mascot, "37:37:34") replaces the earlier "Lv.20" lock = a feature unlocked at level 20.
- Popup "New Season" (first time seen): flower header, timer "37:37:31", picture of a bronze sunflower trophy statue, text: "Play and collect [pink flower icon] to climb the leaderboard in each event. Aim for the top!", green "GO!" button, red close X.
- "Level 21" button blue (not Hard).

## 5. Design notes
- Hard levels appear to be fixed hand-made boards (same in both builds), while normal levels changed between builds.
- Hard intro popup shows a pass-rate % that changes text/number by build (53.99% vs 58.81%).
- NEW at L20: leaderboard event ("New Season", collect flowers, timed ~37 h) unlocked right after beating L20 (teased by the "Lv.20" lock on Home).
- Long stall at 5/10 shows where this level is hard: look-alike colour pairs on 6-long ropes with very little free space.
