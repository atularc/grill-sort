# B_L000: first session, Levels 1-5 + home/profile (walkthrough version, newer UI)

Source: B_L000 (B = Sort Streak Walkthrough playlist). About 10 min (13 sheets, 241 frames, 2.5 s apart). Frames looked at: start + every frame of sheets 01-13. The video is cropped: no booster bar is in frame in any level; a streamer overlay covers the top-right corner (coin numbers partly hidden).

This is a newer build than the A videos: pink/peach sky background, orders counter below the pot shelf, IQ bar from Level 4, flower-cart win screen with stats, home screen with a scene puzzle. Level contents are close to the A levels but L1 and L2 have fewer orders.

## Session flow (outside the levels)
- Boot: "Oakever Games" logo on blue, then a loading screen (garden with fence and potting shed) with the quote: "Play for 30 minutes daily, you'll sleep better and soundly. - Sort Streak".
- After each win: **home screen**: "Sort Streak" logo, "Scene 1" hexagon picture puzzle (triangle pieces fill in), progress "1/25" after L1, "3/25" after L3 and L4, "5/25" after L5; big blue "Level N" button; top-left cat avatar, top-right settings gear + coins; left side a locked icon (brown, padlock, "Lv.20" or similar).
- After L5 (sheet12-13): toast on home "Reach level 21 to unlock the Daily Challenge" when the locked icon is tapped. Then a **Profile** popup: title "Profile", red X, name "Player_ZOVE3", a 4x4 grid of animal avatars (frog, koala, penguin, sheep, mouse, calico cat, fox, bear, lion, chick, cow, deer ...), green "Confirm" button. The player picks the cow and types a name ("Enter your nic..." placeholder, keyboard, name "sort"), then Confirm. Home again with "Level 6".

## Design notes (whole session)
- Difficulty: L1 3 orders / 3-slot ropes, L2 7 orders, L3 10 orders and 4-slot ropes, L4 11 orders, L5 Hard with 12 orders and 5-slot ropes.
- Feature drip: Hint intro at L3, IQ bar at L4, Rope intro on the Hard L5, Daily Challenge locked until level 21, profile after L5.
- Streak banner from L3 ("2 Streak! You're on fire!", "3 Streak! You've raised your own bar!", "4 Streak! Keep that winning rhythm!"); win screen shows Win Streak from L4.
- Hard banner uses a fail rate ("59.69% fail here."), the A build used a pass rate ("Only 50.54% reached the finish.").
- Win screens praise with stats and percentile ("top 4%"); collection: pots fill a cart, levels fill a 25-piece scene puzzle.
