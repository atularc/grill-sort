# A_L028

Source: A_L028 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist). 57 sheet frames (sheet01 20, sheet02 20, sheet03 17) at 2.5 s = about 2 min 20 s of video. Frames looked at: `A_L028_start.png`, all 3 sheets.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 28", no difficulty label. Coins 3490 at start, 3600 after the win (+110).
- No IQ bar (A's build). Back + Restart buttons.
- Orders "0/11".
- Start banner: "4 Streak! You're on a roll!" (wooden sign, flower with "4").

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes; pots: white (helper), orange, green, blue, light blue, red.
- Rope length 5.
- Flowers (top to bottom):
  - white: ?, red, green, orange
  - orange: orange, blue, green, blue, red (full)
  - green: green, ?, orange
  - blue: blue, orange
  - light blue: ?, light blue, ?, blue, light blue (full)
  - red: light blue, orange
- 21 flowers in 30 slots = 9 empty. **4 mystery "?"** (grey flowers with a white "?") at the start.
- Colours at start: orange, red, green, blue, light blue (+ hidden ones). Later: lilac, yellow, purple, pink.

## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order (shelf): orange, light blue, red, blue, lilac, green, red, purple, blue, yellow, pink (red and blue twice).
- Arriving ropes come full and several carry "?" (e.g. a lilac rope with a "?" in the middle; later yellow/purple/pink ropes with 2 "?"). "?" turn into their real colour when they become the bottom flower.
- Queue end: a second white helper pot appeared at the right; the last pink rope was finished with 2 white ropes beside it.
- Boosters: Undo "FREE", Hint 3, Add Rope 1. None used.
- Messages: "Match the bottom flower to drop here!", "Nice!", "Excellent!", "Incredible!", "Legendary!" (twice).
- First serve came only after about 35 s (14 frames at 0/11): the opening needed a lot of reshuffling.
- About 2 min 15 s to solve; roughly 45 board changes.

## 4. End of level
- "Excellent!" over the bouquet (fireworks), button "Level 29". Coins 3600.
- No other popup.

## 5. Design notes
- Harder than L27: 4 hidden pieces in the opening plus "?" in arrivals; slow opening.
- Win word changes with performance: L27 "Flawless!", L28 "Excellent!" (possibly tied to time/steps; not shown).
