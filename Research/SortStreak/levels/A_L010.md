# A_L010 (Sort Streak, Level 10, Hard)

Source: A_L010 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist). 4 contact sheets, 71 frames (~178 s). All frames looked at.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 10" with a red flame icon + "Hard" under it (stays the whole level). Coins 1040 -> 1280.
- No IQ bar (A version).
- Orders at start: "0/12".
- No start banner visible in this video (B_L010 shows a Hard intro card).

## 2. Starting board
- 7 ropes. Pots: white (helper), pink, blue, lilac, yellow, green, red.
- Rope length 5.
- Flowers top to bottom:
  1. white: green
  2. pink: pink, ?, lilac, pink, red
  3. blue: lilac, blue, pink, blue, red
  4. lilac: blue, ?, pink, red
  5. yellow: yellow, green, yellow, lilac, pink
  6. green: yellow, green, blue, lilac, red
  7. red: red
- 26 flowers, 2 "?". Colours: green, pink, lilac, red, blue, yellow (6). Orange, purple, light blue arrive later.
- Free space: 9 empty slots (35 total), but 4 of 7 ropes are full at the start.

## 3. How it was solved
- Red served first (1/12, ~12 s), then a long stall at 1/12 (~60 s). Then pink (2/12), blue (3/12), yellow (4/12), lilac (5/12), green (6/12), orange (7/12), purple/light blue (8-9/12), blue, yellow, red last (12/12).
- Refills full, with "?". End: 2 empty white ropes.
- Boosters: Undo "FREE", Hint 3, Add Rope 1. Not used.
- Messages: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (sheet 3).
- Praise: "Nice!", "Great!", "Excellent!", "Amazing" (small), "Unstoppable!", "Legendary!".
- ~165 s of play.

## 4. End of level
- "Outstanding!" + bouquet, blue "Level 11" button. Coins 1040 -> 1280 (+240 = double the usual +120: Hard reward x2).

## 5. Design notes
- FIRST "Hard" level (Level 10). Same board as B_L010.
- Hard comes from 26 flowers in 35 slots (4 full ropes), 6 colours at once, look-alikes pink/lilac/purple and blue/light blue. Only 2 "?" at the start.
- Hard pays x2 coins (240 vs 120).
