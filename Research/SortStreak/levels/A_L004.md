# A_L004: Level 4

Source: A_L004 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist). About 2 min 25 s (sheet01 20, sheet02 20, sheet03 18 frames). Frames looked at: start + all 58 sheet frames.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 4". No difficulty label. Coins 240 at start, 280 then 350 at the end.
- IQ bar: none visible.
- Orders: "0/11".
- No start banner.

## 2. Starting board
- 7 ropes. Pots: white (helper), lavender, purple, orange, pink, red, blue.
- Rope length 4.
- Flowers top to bottom:
  - White: orange, red, pink, light blue
  - Lavender: empty
  - Purple: purple, red, orange, blue
  - Orange: pink, pink, blue, lavender
  - Pink: purple, lavender, red (3)
  - Red: red, orange, red, blue
  - Blue: blue, lavender, lavender (3)
- Colours on board: orange, red, pink, light blue, purple, blue, lavender (7). Later: green, yellow.
- Empty slots: 4 + 1 + 1 = 6.
- No mystery or blockers. Look-alikes on board: purple / lavender / pink, blue / light blue, red / orange.

## 3. How it was solved
- Served order: blue (1/11, "Nice!"), lavender (2/11), red (3/11, "Great!"), pink (4/11), light blue (5/11), orange (6/11), green (7/11, "Excellent!"), purple ("Amazing!"), yellow, blue again (9/11, "Unstoppable!"), red again (10/11 -> 11/11, "Legendary!").
- Repeats: blue and red each ordered twice.
- Arrivals: full 4-flower mixed ropes at the right end (e.g. yellow rope: yellow, blue, blue, green; blue rope with blue, blue, green; green rope). After the queue ends, white helper ropes fill in (end: white, red, white).
- Messages quoted: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (dragging a stack too big) and "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (several times around 3/11 and 5/11).
- Boosters: Undo ("FREE"), Hint (3), locked "Lv.5". The Hint tile looked pressed/highlighted once around 3/11 but the count stays 3; no clear booster use.
- Hesitation: long stalls at 2/11 (~20 frames) and 3/11 (~20 frames), with a demo hand appearing once (drag hint drawn by the game at 3/11: hand over pink/red stack). Several rejected drops.
- About 45 to 55 moves, ~2 min 20 s.

## 4. End of level
- "Outstanding!", bouquet, confetti. Next button is now **red** "Level 5" with a small "Hard" label (flame icon) under it and a coin badge "x2" on its corner. Coins 350.
- No popup.

## 5. Design notes
- Harder than L3: 7 ropes, 7 colours at start incl. 3 look-alike groups, 11 orders, two repeated colours.
- New: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." rejection; the win screen teases the next level's difficulty ("Hard" + "x2" coins on the red button).
- The level ends with a long streak of praise as the board collapses (each serve frees space).
