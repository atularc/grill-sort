# B_L040 (Level 40)

Source: B_L040 (B = Sort Streak Walkthrough playlist). 5 sheets, about 90 frames x 2.5 s = about 3 min 45 s. Frames looked at: start image, sheet01-05 (all).

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 40" with red flame + **"Hard"**. Coins 569 at start, 580 at the end.
- IQ bar 40 / 90 / IQ / 180; ends at about 90+, badge red; "IQ GENIUS!" sunflower popup shown.
- Orders "0/11".
- Start banner: not visible in the frames (the video starts on the board; no "Hard" fail-rate ribbon or streak banner captured).

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes. Pots: white (helper), light blue, lilac, red, blue, pink.
- Rope length: **6 slots**.
- Flowers top to bottom:
  1. white: lilac
  2. light blue: light blue, blue, red, lilac, red, blue
  3. lilac: lilac, blue, light blue, red, blue
  4. red: lilac, red, light blue, pink, light blue
  5. blue: blue, pink, blue, pink, light blue, pink
  6. pink: pink, lilac, red, pink, light blue
- 28 pieces in 36 slots = 8 empty. **No "?" pieces**, no blockers.
- Only 5 colours at the start and they are look-alike pairs: blue / light blue, pink / red, lilac. Later ropes bring purple, green, orange, yellow, sunflower.

## 3. How it was solved
- **Attempt 1 (failed):** pink served (1/11, "Nice!"), blue (2/11). Then the board filled up (red rope x2, orange, purple+green ropes) and the player got stuck. Bottom bar text: "No Available Moves" / "Use a booster to continue or reset". Popup **"Level Reset"** (red header, X close): "Level progress: 2/11. Restarting will reset it. Restart anyway?" buttons "Restart" / "Cancel". The player pressed Restart (sheet02 #12).
- **Attempt 2:** the same starting board again (restart keeps the layout). Serve order: pink (1/11), blue (2/11), light blue, lilac (4/11), red (5/11), red (6/11), green, ..., purple (8/11, "Excellent!"), blue, orange ("Amazing!"), yellow -> 11/11. Two red orders in a row.
- New ropes arrive full (6 pieces), no "?".
- Two white helper ropes at the end.
- Boosters: no booster bar visible in any frame (the stuck message mentions boosters, but none are shown on screen).
- Messages: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (sheet02 #7), "Nice!", "Excellent!", "Amazing!".
- Long hesitation in attempt 1 (sheet02 #0-#10 at 2/11) and in attempt 2 at 5/11 (sheet04 #0-#17, about 45 s).
- Attempt 2: Steps 66, time 02:19.

## 4. End of level
- "IQ GENIUS!" sunflower popup; coins 569 -> 580 (+11).
- Leaderboard: "Number one! Every effort was worth it!", podium 175 / 245 (player) / 175, "Amazing!" stamp on row 4.
- "Perfect!" flower cart, stats "Time 02:19", "Steps 66", **"Win Streak 0"** (the restart broke the streak).

## 5. Design notes
- Hard without any "?" or blocker: it is hard because of colour look-alikes (only blue / light blue / lilac / red / pink at the start), long 6-slot ropes, only 8 free slots, and duplicate red orders that clog the row.
- New in this set: the **stuck state** ("No Available Moves" / "Use a booster to continue or reset") and the **"Level Reset"** confirm dialog. Restart replays the identical layout. A restart sets Win Streak to 0.
- Good UX to copy: the reset dialog states current progress ("Level progress: 2/11") before you throw it away.
