# B_L017

Source: B_L017 (B = Sort Streak Walkthrough playlist), video 126 s, frames looked at: start image + sheet01 (20), sheet02 (20), sheet03 (10).

NOTE: this is a newer/different build of the game than A_L017. Same level number and same order count (13), but a DIFFERENT starting board, a pink-sky background, an IQ bar, and a different win sequence. The booster bar is cropped out of this recording (not visible).

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 17", no difficulty label. Coins "157x" at start (cropped by a streamer overlay), "170x" at the end (~+130).
- IQ bar: yes, above the top shelf, milestones "40", "90", IQ badge, "180". Fills a small step per served pot; at 13/13 it is about one third full (just past the 40 mark area); the IQ badge flashes red/pink at serves.
- Orders counter: "0/13" (below the top shelf in this build, not above it).
- Banner at start: none visible. The top shelf border flashes pink/red when a pot lands.

## 2. Starting board
- 7 ropes. Pots left to right: white (helper), purple, yellow, orange, green, lavender, red.
- Rope length: 5 slots.
- Flowers, top to bottom:
  - white helper: yellow, orange, yellow, lavender, red (helper starts FULL)
  - purple: purple, ?, orange, purple
  - yellow: yellow, green, green, orange
  - orange: green, purple, red
  - green: ?, lavender, purple, orange, purple
  - lavender: lavender, green, orange, red
  - red: red, lavender, red, lavender
- Colours at start: yellow, orange, lavender, red, purple, green (6). 2 "?" at start.
- Free space: 1 + 1 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 6 empty slots.
- Blockers: 2 mystery "?" only. No vines.

## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order: red (1/13), lavender (2/13), yellow (3/13), orange (4/13), green (5/13), purple (6/13), blue (7/13), then pink, light blue, red, yellow, blue ... to 13/13.
- New ropes arrive full on the right; several bring "?" (blue rope "blue, yellow, green, blue, ?", later ropes with 2-3 "?"). Row shrank to 4, then 2 ropes at the end.
- Rejection: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (sheet01 frame 10).
- Praise: "Incredible!", "Nice!", "Great!", "Incredible!".
- Hesitation: stuck at 7/13 for about 30 s (sheet02 bottom row): many pink/blue/light blue/purple pieces and 3 "?" on the right ropes.
- Steps 62 (from the win screen), time 01:50.

## 4. End of level
- 1) "IQ GENIUS!" splash: a sunflower in a pot wearing glasses and a graduation cap, light bulb above.
- 2) "Flawless!" title with a flower cart (shelves of potted flowers; the cart gains pots = collection/decor progress).
- 3) Stats row: "Time 01:50", "Steps 62", "Win Streak 1"; Time and Steps have a small "S" badge. Text: "Time Wizard! Your time beats 99% of players!"
- Next-level button: not visible within the recording.

## 5. Design notes
- Differs from A_L017: different board (2 "?" here vs none in A), less free space (6 vs 9), different serve order. Both use a full helper rope at the start and 13 orders, so the level's "recipe" (order count, rope count, length) stayed while the layout was regenerated/changed between builds.
- NEW (vs A build): IQ bar with 40/90/180 milestones, "IQ GENIUS!" mascot splash, flower cart collection on the win screen, stats (time, steps, win streak) with a percentile line ("beats 99% of players").
- UX worth copying: percentile praise based on time; win streak counter on the result screen.
