# A_L019

Source: A_L019 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist), video 128 s, frames looked at: start image + sheet01 (20), sheet02 (20), sheet03 (11).

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 19", no difficulty label. Coins 2270 at start, 2390 at the end (+120).
- IQ bar: not visible.
- Orders counter: "0/12".
- Banner at start: "3 Streak! You're on a roll!" (wooden sign with a "3" flower badge).

## 2. Starting board
- 7 ropes. Pots left to right: white (helper), purple, blue, orange, yellow, red, green.
- Rope length: 4 slots.
- Flowers, top to bottom:
  - white helper: yellow, ?, purple
  - purple: purple, green, purple, green
  - blue: covered by a VINE with a badge showing a purple flower (red/orange tips behind)
  - orange: covered by a VINE with a badge showing a blue flower
  - yellow: empty
  - red: ?, red, blue, purple
  - green: green
- Colours at start: yellow, purple, green, red, blue (+ hidden). 2 "?" at start.
- Free space: helper 1 + yellow 4 + green 3 = 8 empty slots (plus the 2 vine ropes).
- Blockers: 2 vines (purple badge, blue badge), 2 "?". The blue-badge vine sits next to the purple-badge one, so they open in a chain: purple served -> first vine opens; blue served -> second opens.

## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order: purple (1/12) -> vine on the blue rope burst (sheet01 frame 8), green (2/12), blue (3/12) -> the second vine gone by 3/12, light blue (4/12), yellow (5/12), orange (6/12), blue again (7/12), red (8/12), pink (9/12), lavender/yellow/red to 12/12.
- New ropes arrive full; the late ones carry many "?" (at 8/12 six "?" across the row, ropes like "?, ?, red", "?, ?, lavender"). Row shrank to 5, 3, 2 ropes at the end.
- Boosters: Undo "FREE", Hint 3, Add Rope 1. None used.
- Rejection: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (twice: sheet01 frame 19, sheet02 frame 11).
- Praise: "Excellent!", "Amazing!", "Incredible!", "Unstoppable!", "Legendary!".
- Hesitation: ~15 s at 8/12 when the mystery-heavy ropes arrived.
- About 50 moves, ~115 s.

## 4. End of level
- Dark overlay, fireworks, "On fire!" title, bouquet. Button first greyed, then red "Level 20" with "Hard" sublabel and coin "x2" tag. Coins 2390.
- No other popup visible.

## 5. Design notes
- Two chained vines (the badge colours unlock in order) is new vs L14-18 (one vine or two independent ones).
- Late-queue "?" density ramps up (6 "?" visible at 8/12): difficulty back-loaded into the queue.
- Win title "On fire!" matches the streak theme; the next Hard level is advertised again with x2 coins.
