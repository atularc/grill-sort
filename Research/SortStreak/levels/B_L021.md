# B_L021

Source: B_L021 (B = Sort Streak Walkthrough playlist), video 107 s, frames looked at: `B_L021_start.png`, `B_L021_sheet01.png` (20), `B_L021_sheet02.png` (20), `B_L021_sheet03.png` (3).

**Big difference from A_L021: this is a different build of the game.** Same level number, but a different board (0/11 instead of 0/12, no "?" flowers), an IQ bar, a pink sky background, a Home screen with a scene puzzle, a streak banner, a "Brilliant!" stats win screen and a leaderboard. A's build had none of these.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 21", no difficulty label. Coins shown "233.." (cut off by the recording overlay; 4th digit not visible), "244.." at the end.
- IQ bar above the pot shelf with milestones "40", "90", "IQ" icon, "180". Nearly empty at start; at 11/11 the IQ icon turns red/pink (milestone reached); exact fill not readable.
- Orders counter at start: "0/11" (shown under the shelf in this build).
- Before the level: Home screen ("Sort Streak" logo, "Scene 1" hexagon picture puzzle "20/25", a timed event icon "37:37:15", settings, button "Level 21").
- Banner at level start (dark overlay): golden trophy with flowers, "2 Streak!" and "Keep that winning rhythm!".

## 2. Starting board
- 7 ropes. Pots left to right: white (helper), blue, red, blue, pink, red, purple.
- Rope length: 4 slots each.
- Flowers, top to bottom:
  1. white: blue, blue, red, pink
  2. blue: pink, blue, red, purple
  3. red: pink, purple, red
  4. blue: blue
  5. pink: wrapped in a clover VINE with a disc "2"; flowers under it hidden (pink, red, blue petals peek out; looks full)
  6. red: red, blue, red, blue
  7. purple: purple, red
- Colours at start: blue, red, pink, purple (4). Light blue, green, orange, yellow, lilac arrive later.
- Free space: about 6 empty slots.
- Blockers: 1 vine with counter "2". No "?" flowers.

## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order: red (1/11), blue (2/11), purple, ... red (4/11, "Amazing!"), blue, light blue, lilac (7/11, "Legendary!"), green (8/11), ... orange (10/11, "Legendary" then "Legendary x4"), 11/11. Red and blue came back.
- New ropes arrive full with a new pot (light blue, green, orange, yellow, lilac seen). When the queue ran out, the row shrank down to the white helper ropes.
- Vine: "2" -> "1" after the first serve, gone after the second serve (same rule as A).
- Boosters: not visible (bottom of the screen is cut off in these frames).
- Messages: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (at 1/11), "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (at 7/11), "Amazing!", "Legendary!", "Legendary x4" (combo of serves in a row).
- Rough moves: the win screen says "Steps 46"; time "01:08".

## 4. End of level
- Board empties, then a full-screen "IQ GENIUS!" splash (smiling sunflower in a pot wearing a graduation cap, light bulb).
- Win screen "Brilliant!": a flower cart that fills with potted flowers, stats tiles "Time 01:08", "Steps 46", "Win Streak 3", text "Time Dominator! Your time left 99% of players behind!".
- Leaderboard screen "Leaderboard" with a timer (37:35:43), top 3 (VagueMédite.. 77, CodeÉtoilé 78, HeartSeeker 77, each with a gift box), player row "sort" goes from rank 50 (6 flowers) to rank 47 (11 flowers), toast "Closer to the top! You climbed 3 places!".
- Coins 233x -> 244x. Back to Home ("Scene 1", 20/25, button "Level 22").

## 5. Design notes
- Easier than A's version: 4 colours at start, no "?", 11 orders; the vine is the only blocker.
- NEW vs. A build: IQ bar, win streak banner + streak stat, combo praise "Legendary xN", time/steps stats with percentile, flower-count leaderboard, Home scene puzzle.
- UI worth copying: streak banner before play, combo counter text, post-level "you beat X% of players" line and leaderboard climb toast.
