# B_L008 (Sort Streak, Level 8)

Source: B_L008 (B = Sort Streak Walkthrough playlist). 2 contact sheets, 39 frames (~98 s). All frames looked at.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 8", no difficulty label. Coins 64x during play, 76x after (digits cut by the facecam overlay).
- IQ bar 40 / 90 / 180. Reaches 180 at the very end -> full-screen "IQ GENIUS!" popup (sunflower in a pot with a graduation cap).
- Orders at start: "0/12".
- Home screen before: "Scene 1" puzzle "6/25" -> "7/25" (one piece added after Level 7), "Level 8" button.
- Start popup: trophy, "7 Streak!" / "Keep that winning rhythm!".

## 2. Starting board
- 7 ropes. Pots: white (helper), yellow, blue, orange, red, lilac, pink.
- Rope length 4.
- Flowers top to bottom:
  1. white: orange, pink, ?, yellow
  2. yellow: yellow
  3. blue: red, blue, red, pink
  4. orange: orange, ?, ?, pink
  5. red: (empty)
  6. lilac: lilac, orange, blue, lilac
  7. pink: ?, yellow, lilac, yellow
- 21 flowers, 4 "?". Colours: orange, pink, yellow, red, blue, lilac (6). Green and purple arrive later.
- Free space: 7 empty slots (28 total).

## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order: yellow (1/12, early, ~15 s), lilac (2/12, "Great" small text), pink/orange (3/12), ... blue (5/12 "Incredible!"), ... red, green, blue, purple at the end.
- New ropes arrive full, some with "?". Queue runs out near the end: board goes down to 2 empty white ropes.
- Boosters: not visible (bottom cropped).
- Praise: "Great", "Amazing!", "Incredible!", "Legendary X3", "Legendary X5" (x-counter grows with a combo of fast serves).
- No rejection message seen.
- Results: Time 01:13, Steps 45.

## 4. End of level
- "IQ GENIUS!" popup, then "Unstoppable!" flower cart. Tiles: "Time 01:13", "Steps 45", "Win Streak 8". Text: "Blazing Speed! Your time was faster than 99% of players!".
- Coins 64x -> 76x (about +120).

## 5. Design notes
- Different starting board from A_L008 (same counts: 7 ropes x 4, 21 flowers, 0/12), see A_L008.
- Fast level (73 s) with a long fast-serve combo: Legendary X3 -> X5.
- NEW vs Level 7: the "IQ GENIUS!" popup when the IQ bar hits 180.
