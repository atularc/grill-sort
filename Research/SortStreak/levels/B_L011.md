# B_L011 (Sort Streak, Level 11)

Source: B_L011 (B = Sort Streak Walkthrough playlist). 3 contact sheets, 56 frames (~140 s). All frames looked at.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 11", no difficulty label. Coins 100x -> 110x (digits cut).
- IQ bar 40 / 90 / 180 (no IQ GENIUS popup this time).
- Orders at start: "0/10".
- Start banner: small pink "Good Luck!" (frame 2). The "Locked Flowers" tutorial is NOT visible in this video. No streak trophy popup either.

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes. Pots: white (helper), purple, red, blue, orange, lilac.
- Rope length 5.
- Flowers top to bottom:
  1. white: LOCKED: green clover vine over the rope, wooden badge with a PURPLE flower.
  2. purple: purple, red, orange, red, purple
  3. red: orange, blue, lilac, ?, purple
  4. blue: ?, lilac, purple
  5. orange: orange, ?, ?, lilac, blue
  6. lilac: ?, purple
- 20 visible flowers, 5 "?". Colours: purple, red, orange, blue, lilac (5).
- Free space on open ropes: 5 slots.

## 3. How it was solved
- Purple served first (1/10, ~28 s): the vine falls off in leaves and the white helper rope opens (empty).
- Serve order: purple (1/10), blue (2/10), orange (3/10), red (4/10, shelf flashes), lilac (5/10), pink (6/10), red/blue (7/10), green, yellow (10/10).
- Refills full, some with "?". End: 2 empty white ropes.
- Boosters not visible.
- Messages: "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (sheet 3).
- Praise: "Good Luck!" (start + again at 2/10), "Nice!", "Great!", "Amazing!", "Incredible!", "Unstoppable!", "Legendary".
- Results: Time 02:06, Steps 56.

## 4. End of level
- "On fire!" flower cart. Tiles "Time 02:06", "Steps 56", "Win Streak 0" (streak reset; no reason shown on screen, the player may have failed/restarted off-video).
- Home: "Scene 1" "10/25", "Level 12" button.

## 5. Design notes
- Different board from A_L011 (A: lock on the yellow customer rope, red key; B: lock on the helper rope, purple key, 5 "?"). Both 6 ropes x 5, 0/10.
- Locking the HELPER rope is a strong squeeze: no free helper until the first purple serve.
- "Good Luck!" banner appears instead of a streak popup when the streak is 0.
