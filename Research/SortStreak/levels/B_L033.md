## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order (from the top shelf): blue, red, green, light blue, blue, (piggy coins, not counted in x/11), red, pink, purple, orange, lilac... ends 11/11. Blue and red each came twice.
- After the first frames the piggy rope collected 4 coins (frame ~sheet02 #9 at 5/11); coins flew up to the level header and the piggy pot left. The counter did not go up for it, so the piggy is a bonus, not an order.
- New ropes arrive with the next pot and full of flowers (often with "?" pieces). When the queue ran out, a second white helper pot appeared at the right end (from 6/11), and later ropes simply left; at the end only 2 white helper ropes were left.
- Vine: badge 2 -> 1 after the first serve (sheet01 #12), gone after the second serve (rope shown with green flowers at 2/11, served at 3/11).
- Boosters: none visible on the board in these frames (no booster bar visible).
- Messages quoted: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." , "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" , praise: "Great!", "Excellent!", "Legendary" (rainbow), plus a praise word in a small banner at 6/11 (not readable).
- Hesitation: several frames with no change in sheet01 (#4-#9) while the player looked at the board. No undo visible.
- Moves: about 30 board changes; solve time about 85 s.

## 5. Design notes
- Hard parts: one rope locked by a vine for the first 2 serves, many "?" pieces, 5-slot ropes, 11 orders.
- New vs earlier levels (for this agent's set): vine blocker with serve countdown; piggy-bank pot that collects coin pieces as a bonus rope (does not count as an order, pays coins at the end with a hammer-smash animation); a second helper rope appears when the queue empties.
- UI: the coin bonus rope is a nice "extra goal" that uses the same sorting; the leaderboard/flower-cart flow is a meta event after each win.
