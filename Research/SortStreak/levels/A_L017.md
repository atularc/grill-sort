## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order: yellow (1/13), light blue (2/13), purple (3/13), pink (4/13), lavender (5/13), green (6/13), blue (7/13), red (8/13), purple again, red, yellow, orange, red ... to 13/13. Red ordered at least 3 times, many red/orange/yellow ropes late in the level.
- New ropes arrive full on the right (blue, red, orange, red, yellow...). No "?" seen on queued ropes. Row shrank to 5, 3, then 2 ropes at the end.
- Boosters: Undo "FREE", Hint 3, Add Rope 1. None used.
- Rejections: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (sheet02 frame 8, faint) and "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (sheet03 frame 5).
- Praise: "Nice!", "Great!", "Excellent!", "Amazing!", "Incredible!".
- Hesitation: stuck at 7/13 for about 30 s (sheet02 bottom row + sheet03 start): board full of red/orange/purple/blue/yellow with very little space.
- About 55 moves, ~155 s.

## 4. End of level
- Dark overlay, "Impressive!" title, bouquet. "Level 18" button first greyed out (sheet04 frame 2), then turns blue: the button is held back for about 1 s.
- Coins 2140. No other popup visible.

## 5. Design notes
- No blockers or "?"; difficulty comes from the long queue (13 orders), a full helper at the start, and red/orange look-alike colours stacking up late (red repeats 3+ times).
- Red vs orange (and blue vs light blue, purple vs lavender) are look-alike pairs the level leans on.
- UX: win button delayed so the player can't skip the celebration.
