## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order (shelf): green ("Nice!", 1/12), blue, red ("Great!", 3/12), yellow ("Excellent!"), purple, blue ("Incredible!"), lilac/yellow ..., pink, light blue, orange, red ... 12 total.
- Vines: at 1/12 the "2" vine on blue #1 is gone (its rope shows flowers) and the other vine shows "2" (was "4"). It stays "2" through 2/12, turns "1" on the red serve (3/12), and breaks (leaf burst) together with "Excellent!" on the 4th serve. What exactly the counter counts is not clear from the frames (probably serves, but the first serve took 4 -> 2).
- New ropes arrive full from the right, later ones with many "?" (sheet02 bottom row: 3-4 "?" per rope).
- Rejection: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (sheet01 f6).
- Praise: "Nice!", "Great!", "Excellent!", "Incredible!", "Legendary x4".
- Win screen: Time 02:01, Steps 62.

## 5. Design notes
- Two vines lock 2 of 6 colour ropes at the start, so space is tight (6 free slots) until they open.
- First numbered vine in this batch (L56 had a colour-icon vine).
- Many "?" in late arrivals keep the end phase hard.
