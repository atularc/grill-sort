# A_L012 (Sort Streak, Level 12)

Source: A_L012 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist). 2 contact sheets, 35 frames (~88 s). All frames looked at.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 12", no difficulty label. Coins 1380 -> 1394 -> 1490.
- No IQ bar (A version).
- Orders at start: "0/11".
- Start banner (wooden sign with a pink flower badge "2"): "2 Streak! You're on a roll!".

- 7 ropes. Pots: white (helper), yellow, red, pink, blue, lilac, blue (two blue pots).
- Rope length 4.
- Flowers top to bottom:

  2. yellow: yellow, blue, ?, blue
  3. red: ?, lilac, pink, yellow

## 3. How it was solved
- Yellow served first (1/11, ~12 s): vine bursts into leaves, the pink rope opens.
- Then blue (2/11), pink (3/11), lilac (4/11), red/purple (5/11), green (6/11), red (7/11), orange, light blue (8-9/11), purple, red last (11/11).
- Refills full, with "?". End: 2 empty white ropes.
- Boosters: Undo "FREE", Hint 3, Add Rope 1. Not used.
- Messages: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (sheet 1).
- Praise: "Nice!", "Excellent!", "Incredible!", "Unstoppable!".

## 4. End of level
- "Awesome!" + bouquet, "Level 13" button. Coins 1380 -> 1490 (+110 total; counter showed 1394 mid-way).

## 5. Design notes
- Second vine level. The unlock colour (yellow) can be finished almost at once (yellow rope + yellow at bottoms).
- Two pots of the same colour (blue) on the shelf at once, both with a single blue flower.
- Quick level (~80 s): a breather after Hard L10 and the new mechanic in L11.
- Player A also gets a streak banner, styled as a wooden sign (B shows a gold trophy).
