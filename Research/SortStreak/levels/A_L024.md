## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order: purple (1/12, "Nice!"), red (2/12), blue (3/12), red (4/12, "Excellent!", then "Amazing!"), yellow (5/12), blue (6/12, "Incredible!"), green (7/12), pink (8/12), yellow (9/12), lilac (9-10/12, "Amazing!"), light blue, orange (12/12). Red, blue and yellow came back.
- Vine: "2" -> "1" around the first serve, burst into leaves at about 1/12-2/12.
- New ropes arrive full, several with "?". From ~8/12 a second white helper rope is in the row (white ... white); at the end only the two white ropes are left.
- Boosters: Undo "FREE", Hint 3, Add Rope 1. None used.
- Messages: **"Mystery flowers can only go on empty ropes."** (at 3/12, the player tried to drop a stack holding a "?" onto a non-empty rope), "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (at 6/12 and at 7/12), praise "Nice!", "Excellent!", "Amazing!", "Incredible!".
- Hesitation: long still stretches at 3/12 and 6/12 (~10 frames each).
- Rough moves: ~40 board changes, ~150 s.
