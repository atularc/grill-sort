# A_L029

Source: A_L029 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist). 47 sheet frames (sheet01 20, sheet02 20, sheet03 7) at 2.5 s = about 2 min of video. Frames looked at: `A_L029_start.png`, all 3 sheets.

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 29", no difficulty label. Coins 3600 at start, 3690 after the last serve, 3720 on the win screen (+120).
- No IQ bar (A's build).
- Orders "0/12".
- Start banner: "5 Streak! You're on a roll!"

## 2. Starting board
- 7 ropes; pots: white (helper), red, orange, yellow, red, yellow, purple (lilac).
- Rope length 5.
- Flowers (top to bottom):
  - white: lilac, orange, red
  - red: red, red, yellow, red
  - orange: orange, yellow, yellow, red, yellow (full)
  - yellow: **clover vine**, wooden sign "2" (contents hidden)
  - red (2nd): empty
  - yellow (2nd): yellow, red, orange, yellow, red (full)
  - lilac: **clover vine**, round badge with a green-flower icon
- 17 visible flowers, 8 empty slots on the 5 open ropes. No "?" at the start; "?" appear on arriving ropes.
- Two pots of the same colour on the board at once (2 red, 2 yellow).

## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order (shelf): red, yellow, orange, green, then red, lilac, light blue, blue, pink, purple, blue ... (12 in total; the second red and yellow pots on the board each needed their own full rope).
- Vines: the "2" vine counted down to "1" after the 1st serve and fell after the 2nd serve. The green-badge vine stayed until a green rope (arrived after the 1st serve) was served as the 4th order; then it opened.
- Arriving ropes are full; green, light blue, blue, pink, purple ropes arrived with 1-3 "?" pieces. Queue end: second white helper at the right, finished with 2 white ropes.
- Boosters: Undo "FREE", Hint 3, Add Rope 1. None used.
- Messages: "Nice!", "Great!", "Incredible!", "Unstoppable!", "Legendary!" (twice), "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (twice, late in the level).
- About 1 min 55 s; a calm stretch at 2/12-3/12 (about 25 s).

## 4. End of level
- "Fantastic!" over the bouquet. The next-level button is **red**: "Level 30" with a flame icon and "Hard" under it, and a coin "x2" badge on its corner (Hard levels pay double).
- No other popup.

## 5. Design notes
- Clover vines again (one count lock, one colour lock, as in B_L026); the colour lock needs a colour that is not yet on the board, so the player must wait for the green rope to arrive.
- Duplicate pot colours on the board at the same time (2 red, 2 yellow) is a new twist in this set.
- The Hard warning appears on the PREVIOUS level's win screen button, with the x2 coin promise.
