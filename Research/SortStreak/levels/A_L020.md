# A_L020

Source: A_L020 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist), video 191 s, frames looked at: start image + sheet01 (20), sheet02 (20), sheet03 (20), sheet04 (16).

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 20" with red "Hard" label (flame icon). Coins 2390 at start, 2590 at the end (+200, same as the L15 Hard level).
- IQ bar: not visible.
- Orders counter: "0/10".
- Banners at start, in order:
  1. Hard intro popup: a red ribbon "Hard" with a white diamond flower icon, and a white panel: "Only 53.99% pass this level. Can you do it?"
  2. "4 Streak! You're on a roll!" (wooden sign, "4" badge).

## 2. Starting board
- 6 ropes (one fewer than L14-L19). Pots left to right: white (helper), green, pink, red, blue, yellow.
- Rope length: 6 slots (longest so far).
- Flowers, top to bottom:
  - white helper: blue, pink, yellow, green, yellow
  - green: green, red, yellow, blue, red, green (full)
  - pink: blue, red, pink, blue, pink, red (full)
  - red: blue, pink, yellow, green
  - blue: red, pink
  - yellow: yellow, green, yellow, green
- Colours at start: blue, pink, yellow, green, red (5). No "?".
- Free space: 1 + 2 + 4 + 2 = 9 empty slots of 36.
- Blockers: none (no vines, no "?").

## 3. How it was solved
- Very long opening: the counter stays at 0/10 for about 70 s (all of sheet01 and the first half of sheet02); the player reshuffles long runs (6 per colour needed).
- Hint: the bulb button is pressed (enlarged, then outlined in yellow, sheet02 frames 3-4); its count stays "3" in later frames, so whether a hint was consumed is not visible.
- Serve order: green (1/10), yellow (2/10), red (3/10), pink (4/10), blue (5/10), orange (6/10), red again (7/10), then light blue, purple, lavender to 10/10.
- New ropes arrive full (6 pieces) on the right: orange, purple, light blue, lavender, red; no "?" seen. Red/orange and blue/light blue and purple/lavender look-alike pairs share the board late. Row shrank to 5, then 2 ropes.
- Boosters: Undo "FREE", Hint 3, Add Rope 1.
- Praise: "Great!", "Excellent!", "Nice!", "Great!", "Amazing!".
- No rejection message seen.
- About 60 moves, ~180 s.

## 4. End of level
- Dark overlay, fireworks, "Awesome!" title, bouquet. Blue "Level 21" button (no sublabel). Coins 2590.
- No other popup visible.

## 5. Design notes
- Hard without any blocker: difficulty comes purely from rope length 6 (6 of a colour per order), only 6 ropes, a nearly full helper (5/6) and fully mixed starting ropes. Shows the order size jump to 6 at L20.
- NEW: Hard intro popup with a pass-rate line ("Only 53.99% pass this level. Can you do it?") — first seen here (L15 Hard showed no such popup in the frames).
- Streak banner now "4 Streak!" (L18 = 2, L19 = 3): the streak counts consecutive wins across levels.
