Source: B_L028 (B = Sort Streak Walkthrough playlist). 54 sheet frames (sheet01 20, sheet02 20, sheet03 14) at 2.5 s = about 2 min 15 s of video. Frames looked at: `B_L028_start.png`, all 3 sheets.

- Arriving ropes come full, several with 1-2 "?" (e.g. a pink rope and a purple rope with "?" in the middle). When the queue ran out a second white helper appeared at the right; ended with 2 white ropes.
- Boosters: not visible in B's frames.
- Messages: "Great!", "This rope can't hold that many flowers.", "Unstoppable!", "Legendary!", "Legendary x2", "Legendary x4".
- Same slow opening as A: about 14 frames (35 s) at 0/11 before the first serve.
- Win screen: Time 01:45, Steps 59.

## 4. End of level

## 5. Design notes
- A and B: same board, similar opening struggle (~35 s before the first serve) and similar total time (A ~2 min 15 s, B 1:45 on the timer).
- Win word differs by player/build: A "Excellent!", B "Impressive!". B's subtitle praises steps instead of time this time ("Puzzle Prodigy!").
- The flower-cart on the win screen gains pots level by level (collection meta).
