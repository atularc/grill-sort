Source: A_L014 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist), video 109 s, frames looked at: start image + sheet01 (20), sheet02 (20), sheet03 (3).

- Flowers, top to bottom:

  - light blue: blue, blue, lavender

Lavender served first -> the purple rope's vine burst (sheet01 frame 5). Light blue/blue served -> green rope's vine gone by 2/11.

## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order (top shelf): lavender (1/11), light blue (2/11), red (3/11), blue (4/11), green (5/11), yellow (6/11), purple (7/11), red again (8/11), then pink, orange, blue/orange to 11/11. Red came back twice; blue came twice.
- New ropes arrive at the right end after each serve, already full: yellow rope (red, yellow, blue, red), pink rope arriving with "?, ?, ?, pink" (3 mystery pieces), orange rope with "?" pieces. When the queue ran out, the row shrank (only 3-4 ropes at 8/11-10/11).
- "?" pieces revealed as they became the bottom flower.
- Boosters: Undo "FREE" (greyed until first move), Hint 3, Add Rope 1. None used (counts unchanged).
- Praise words: "Nice!", "Great!", "Incredible!", "Unstoppable!", "Legendary!" (escalating during the fast end run).
- No rejection message seen. No visible undo.
- About 40 moves, ~105 s.

## 4. End of level
- Dark overlay with confetti, "Brilliant!" title, a large flower bouquet (all served colours) with butterflies.
- Button: red "Level 15" with a small "Hard" sublabel (flame icon) and a coin "x2" tag on its corner. Coins 1700.
- No other popup visible.

## 5. Design notes
- Hard parts: two vines hiding whole ropes until a specific colour is served; 2 "?" at start plus 3 "?" on a queued rope; red and blue orders repeat.
- NEW vs earlier: vine blocker with colour badge (condition = serve that colour). The next-level button announces the next level's difficulty ("Hard").
- UX: praise words escalate with a streak of quick serves; win button previews the next level's difficulty and coin doubling.
