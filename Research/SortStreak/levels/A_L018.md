Source: A_L018 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist), video 165 s, frames looked at: start image + sheet01 (20), sheet02 (20), sheet03 (20), sheet04 (6).

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 18", no difficulty label. Coins 2140 at start, 2270 at the end (+130).
- IQ bar: not visible.
- Orders counter: "0/13".
- Banner at start: a wooden sign with a "2" flower badge: "2 Streak! You're on a roll!" (first frame, fades out).

## 2. Starting board
- 7 ropes. Pots left to right: white (helper), lavender, pink, yellow, purple, red, orange.
- Rope length: 4 slots.
- Flowers, top to bottom:
  - white helper: empty
  - lavender: lavender, purple, pink, lavender
  - pink: red, yellow, lavender
  - yellow: covered by a VINE with a badge showing a lavender flower
  - purple: purple
  - red: orange, red, pink, lavender
  - orange: red, ?, orange, purple

- Free space: helper 4 + pink 1 + purple 3 = 8 empty slots (plus the vine rope).
- Blockers: 1 vine (lavender badge), 1 mystery "?".

## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order: lavender (1/13) -> vine on the yellow rope burst (sheet01 frame 8), purple (2/13), yellow (3/13), orange (4/13), pink (5/13), red (6/13), red again (7/13), blue (8/13), blue/light blue/green/yellow/purple to 13/13. Red and blue ordered twice; blue + light blue on the row together.
- New ropes arrive full on the right, some with 1-3 "?" (e.g. blue rope "green, ?, ?, ?" region at 4/13). Row shrank to 5, 4, then 3 ropes.
- Boosters: Undo "FREE", Hint 3, Add Rope 1. None used.
- Rejection: "Match the bottom flower to drop here!" (sheet02 frame 10).
- Praise: "Unstoppable!", "Legendary!" (twice).
- Hesitation: long stretches at 2/13 and 4/13 (~15 s each) and 8/13 (~15 s).
- About 55 moves, ~155 s.

## 4. End of level
- Dark overlay, fireworks, "Excellent!" title, bouquet; blue "Level 19" button. Coins 2270.
- No other popup visible.
