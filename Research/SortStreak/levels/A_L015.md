# A_L015

Source: A_L015 (A = Beyond Gamer playlist), video 128 s, frames looked at: start image + sheet01 (20), sheet02 (20), sheet03 (11).

## 1. Header / HUD
- "Level 15" with a red "Hard" label (flame icon) under it. Coins 1700 at start, 1900 at the end (+200).
- IQ bar: not visible.
- Orders counter: "0/10".
- Banner at start: none visible.

## 2. Starting board
- 7 ropes. Pots left to right: white (helper), blue, red, lavender, red, green, purple. Two red pots on the row at once.
- Rope length: 5 slots (longer than L14's 4).
- Flowers, top to bottom:
  - white helper: green, red, green (the helper rope starts with flowers on it)
  - blue: covered by a VINE with a badge showing a red flower (contents hidden)
  - red: ?, lavender, blue, ?, red
  - lavender: purple, blue, ?, ?, red
  - red: red, red, green, blue
  - green: red, ?, red
  - purple: lavender, purple, ?, purple, green
- Colours at start: green, red, lavender, blue, purple (+ hidden under vine). 6 "?" on the board.
- Free space (visible ropes): helper 2, red rope 1, green rope 2 = about 5 empty slots, plus the vine rope.
- Blockers: 1 vine (red badge), 6 mystery "?".

## 3. How it was solved
- Serve order: red (1/10) -> the vine on the blue rope burst (sheet01 frame 9-10), blue (2/10), lavender (3/10), purple (4/10), red again (5/10), green (6/10), yellow (7/10), light blue (8/10), orange and pink to 10/10.
- New ropes arrive full on the right: yellow, orange, pink, light blue; several carry "?" pieces (e.g. a rope with "?, yellow, ?, ..."). When the queue ended the row shrank to 4, then 2 ropes.
- Boosters: Undo "FREE", Hint 3, Add Rope 1. None used.
- Rejection: "This rope can't hold that many flowers." (sheet02 frame 4).
- Praise: "Great!", "Excellent!", "Unstoppable!", "Legendary!".
- Several still frames in sheet01 (0/10 for ~20 s) = player thinking at the start.
- About 45 moves, ~115 s.

## 4. End of level
- Dark overlay, confetti, "Perfect!" title, bouquet with butterflies.
- Blue button "Level 16" (no difficulty sublabel, no x2 tag). Coins 1900 (+200 for a Hard level vs +110 for L14).
- No other popup visible.

## 5. Design notes
- Hard because: rope length 5, 6 "?" at start plus more on queued ropes, two red orders on screen at once, very little free space (helper already partly filled), vine hides a rope until red is served.
- NEW: first "Hard" label in this set; Hard pays more coins (~200). Win button colour/label change by next level's difficulty (red + "Hard" + x2 before a Hard level, plain blue otherwise). Win title varies ("Brilliant!" on L14, "Perfect!" here).
- Helper rope starting pre-filled is a cheap way to cut slack.
